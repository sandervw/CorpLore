# Scene Frame — Scene 6

## Scene Parameters

**Senses:** sight, touch, pain
The fax-room is read through exhausted eyes and aching wounds — Igon sees the cave-painted walls and constellation rug; he feels the cold stone floor under him and the throb of his forearm cut; pain keeps him present

**Rhythms:** periodic-with-interruptions, cumulative
Guilliam's monologue builds clause on clause toward its gloating summit, but his own self-interruptions splice the periodicity — long structured sentences that derail mid-arc and restart

**Scene shape:** escalation
Guilliam's confidence and dominion mount with each speech turn; Igon's inner state declines in parallel. The final silence is the ceiling of both arcs.

**Color palette:** violets-and-yellows
Yellow-and-silver filing cabinets, the sickly amber of ancient indicator lights; the violet of deep bruising and shadowed stucco, the dim purple of the constellation rug. Together they read as archaic, semi-sacred

## Source Imprint

**Sentence-length distribution:** Short declarations (3-8 words) alternate with long accumulative sentences (30-50 words); almost no middle range. The short lines land like blows.

**Clause stacking:** Right-branching and cumulative; long sentences pile subordinate clauses and participial phrases after the main verb. Periodic constructions are used sparingly, for climax only.

**Register and vocabulary:** Plain, direct, occasionally archaic in diction ("unconscionable," "suffused," "adherents"). No contractions. Latinate words reserved for the ancient entity's speech; human characters speak plainer.

**Dominant sensory channels:** Sight dominates; motion described as visual spectacle. Sound appears at peaks (the vast voice, the hundred reports).

**Signature tics:** Exclamatory speech introduced with quotation-length cries. Repetition of proper names for grandeur ("Rogol Domedonfors"). Action verbs front-loaded in participial clauses.

**Do:** Open with short declarative statements. Let the ancient entity speak in self-naming, boastful periods. Use plain verbs for physical action; ornament only the entity's voice.
**Don't:** Mid-range sentences of 12-20 words. Hedged or qualified speech from the entity. Adverb clusters.

---

## Speech Rules

### Guilliam

- **Positive mode:** Interrupts itself mid-sentence and restarts on a different track; answers questions with warnings or refusals rather than information. Cap: fires on ~50% of Guilliam's lines.
- **Register:** Technical, jargon-heavy, formal-modern. No contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 25 words. Ends lines on a noun wherever possible.
- **Signature construction:** Begins most lines with a conjunction (And / But / Or). Cap: ~50% of lines.
- **Default-banned:** Warm or emotive language; "what does it feel like"-type questions; fragment-as-style.

*Note:* Igon does not speak in this scene.

---

## Forbidden Patterns

- No metaphors built on weather or seasons
- No similes whose vehicle is a household item
- No `X, then Y, then Z` action sequences
- No negative-construction sentences ("he did not X")
- No silence described as a physical weight
- No rain-on-stone or rain-on-armor imagery
- No sententious closing lines (one-line moralisms)
- No interiority for Igon beyond exhaustion and grief — do not explain what he thinks about the revelation; let posture and stillness carry it
- Do not let Guilliam express warmth, regret, or sympathy
- Do not resolve the ambiguity of Igon's fate
- Do not describe the hold-music or oboe as beautiful or comforting in retrospect
