# Speech Rules — Igon

- **Positive mode:** Warm exclamatory opener ("Ohh," / "Aha!") + greeting; self-deprecating "Hah!" after grandiosity or awkwardness.
- **Register:** Slightly formal, old-fashioned, courtly-but-conversational. Sparing contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 20 words. Short exclamations and ellipsis-trailed fragments allowed at emotional peaks.
- **Signature construction:** Requests framed as invitations / rhetorical questions.
- **Default-banned:** Commands or imperatives; modern slang; cynicism; mockery aimed outward.
