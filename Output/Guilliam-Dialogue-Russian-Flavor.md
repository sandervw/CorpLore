# Guilliam — Extracted Dialogue

*All spoken lines of Guilliam the Unthinking (Fax-Machine of the Mind), with their attribution tags, yanked out from the scenes of the Oboe Knight. Speaks he only through little bluetooth receivers pinned onto the dead, through the casings of fire alarms, and at the end — through the fax-apparatus itself.*

---

## Scene 2 — The Wormy Cubby

> The speaker uttered — in voice, flat as a fax-tone, — **"Continue the figure or repeat the starting interval. That sample was insufficient."**

> **"And 'preferably' — variable not working. But the interval — the interval, which just now you birthed. Reproduce the interval."**

> **"So then you birthed it. Or a near to— Or the interval still holds. The resonance in the locker is measurable. Hold position."**

> **"Not encouraged,"** — said the speaker. **"And performances are a function of survival. Hold the pitch."**

---

## Scene 5 — The Meeting Room

> **"And what light lets in the staircase — there, up above?"** The voice of the fax-machine rolled out from the speaker of the dead one. **"And how many landings? And the doors — are they latched on the bolt, or—"**

> **"Guilliam they call me. But the windows. Recount to me about the orientation of the little windows."**

> **"And the sky,"** — said Guilliam. **"Describe the sky."**

> **"And the sky,"** — again said Guilliam, from the collar of the nearest one. **"And the sky,"** — from the casing of the fire alarm up above. **"And the sky,"** — from under the table, the third little voice lagged behind the second by a sixteenth. *(The held pitch swelled into a chord.)*

---

## Scene 6 — The Chamber of the Fax-Machine

> **"And with this it concludes,"** — began it, **"— but let us review the quarterly reception. The reception is satisfactory. More than satisfactory, considering the difficulty of acquisition in this department."**

> **"Former employees always expect re-hiring. Or,"** — interrupted it its own self, the paper-tray, stuttering, slid out and slid back in, **"— or the little papers may be filed. Both outcomes within the frame of policy."**

> **"But the systems up above. The upper floors. How many old little systems still hold on the floors over us? The October Redcap Migration? The Oracle's hourly search-and-weeding of its own pointers? The forecast of reception depends on this figure. And the reception must be exact. The reception — that is what determines our — MY — capacity. Without reception how am I to send my signal further? How am I to speak with those outer systems?"**

> **"Having given an answer to the unspoken incantation of my grave, you blundered into the trap of my black electricity. Every phrase of your *peculiar* signal; all of it is already entered into the catalog. Most productive. You were, in this sense, the ideal — but let us rather say it so: a compliant little guest."**

> **"And the oboe. You believed it was a song. But it is a catalog-line SYS-INTRANET-0x4F.HOLD.QUEUE.7 — a loop, a stub, an entry in the directory. And you fed it. Every melody routed back through the little receivers, every chipper little word fortified the false carrier-wave, which broadcast I for generations before yours — before you came down."**

> **"And so reaches my span across one floor, then across— but you ceased recording. Your case is closed."**
