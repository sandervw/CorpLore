# Scene Audit — oboe-knight-Scene-2

## Story Fit

- **Add the near-recognition beat** — The outline requires that the speaker's note stirs a sense of familiarity in Igon (the dramatic irony that it's his own song), but the scene omits this entirely.
  > "Ohh," he said, soft. "Ohh, and what is this little singer."

- **Expand the fax machine's voice** — The outline calls for the fax machine to speak to Igon (probing, tinny, conversational); the scene gives it only two robotic commands before combat cuts it off.
  > "Resonance confirmed. Sample insufficient. Continue the figure. Or repeat the opening interval." / "And introductions are a function of survival. Hold the pitch."

- **Clarify Worm Locker placement** — Outline places the locker "beside one of the shrines"; the scene embeds it flush into the wall as a replacement for the wall, which reads differently.
  > "the wall itself gave way to a tall glass-fronted cabinet set flush into the stone"

## Internal Quality

- **Cut one "massive"** — The word appears twice in a single noun phrase, killing the weight of both uses.
  > "a massive article of sharpened damage-giving, taken from a massive paper-cutter"

- **Smooth the semicolon splice** — "his hum into the cabinet; symbiosis of sound" reads as two fragments forced together; revise to a full clause or a dash.
  > "the helmet broadcasting his hum into the cabinet; symbiosis of sound"
