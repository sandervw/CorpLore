# Igon — Speech Excerpted

*All words by Igon (the "Oboe Knight") of Allsoden uttered, with their attribution-titles, from scenes of the Oboe Knight drawn out. Through the iron of his helm by voice he speaks, cheerful and unafraid, until to the final chamber it is come — where nothing at all he says.*

---

## Scene First — The Entering-In into the Sub-Cellar

> **"Oho, what a pleasant old hall,"** into the iron of his helm he said.

> **"The Manual tells the Westlake Companey, through so many these ages, this place to itself to have left,"** he said. *(...)* **"Great courtesy. Every hallowed gesture I shall make which — I know — to the old wardens, in such a sub-cellar hired, may give satisfaction."**

> **"Hmm,"** said Igon. **"Hmm."**

> **"Aha, and to thee likewise greeting, late friend! Thy little secret to me hast thou then revealed?"**

> **"How splendid. Well between us now known we are."**

---

## Scene Second — The Cabinet of the Worm

> **"How courteous a salutation,"** said Igon. *(...)* **"This most recent post the throng with new faces to furnish I see."**

> **"Oho,"** he said, softly. **"Oho, and who is this little singer?"**

> **"Hmm, might it be permitted to ask what kind of figure is preferred?"** said Igon.

> **"Hah!"** said Igon. **"A fair demand. Though I confess myself the tune itself not to remember."**

> **"And might I ask what is being measured, friend, or is that inquiry discouraged?"**

> **"Ahh, little singer,"** said Igon. **"How brief a recital."**

---

## Scene Third — The Four Cubicles of the Servants

> **"Oho, what a pleasant station,"** said Igon. **"And I for my part do believe the singing in this place nearer to be. The oboe. Hither I came that that sweet and soft music I might find, whereby it I might accompany, nor it in dream alone to sing might I suffer until it vanish."**

> **"Ahh. Then perhaps another custom."**

> **"Friends, if it must be fought, I shall comply."**

> **"Oho — oho, alas. A warden so hedged-about I had not foreseen."**

> **"Ahh. Against me bolted. Hah."**

---

## Scene Fourth — The Descent

> **"Oho, ancient passage,"** into the iron he murmured, **"a vocal host thou art. More welcome than accustomed I am, I fear."**

---

## Scene Fifth — The Council Chamber

> **"Mayest thou pardon this necessary violence,"** panting Igon said.

> **"Oho, friend below,"** cried Igon, his breath between blows held suspended, **"such a blazing light far off is..."**

---

## Scene Sixth — The Chamber of the Copying-Machine

> *(No speech. Igon with legs crossed silent sits — his whisper snuffed out "as though by a worn-down diode" — nor ever to Guilliam does he answer.)*
