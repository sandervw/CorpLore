# Scene Audit — Scene 3

## Story Fit

- **Add bluetooth speakers to the risen dead** — The outline specifies the fax machine animates the dead via bluetooth speaker-receivers clipped to them; Scene 4 hinges on Igon realizing this. None of the eleven risen dead in Scene 3 are described with speakers, severing that setup.
  > "Three Contractually Obligated leaned against the far wall, brass clasps glinting over their breastbones."

- **Clarify that humming drew the dead** — The outline states the dead are drawn "like moths toward a musical flame" (the draft uses this exact phrase), but the dead begin stirring before Igon hums, making the cause ambiguous. The fax machine's necromancy is supposed to respond to his resonance.
  > "Unbeknownst to Igon, however, within each server room, the Contractually Obligated had indeed stirred."
