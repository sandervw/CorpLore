# Igon — Extracted Dialogue

*All spoken lines of Igon ("the Oboe Knight") of Allsode, with their attribution tags, drawn from the Oboe Knight scenes. He speaks aloud through his iron helm, jovial and undaunted, until the final chamber — where he says nothing at all.*

---

## Scene 1 — Entry into the Sub-Basement

> **"Ohh, what a lovely old hallway,"** he said into the iron of his helmet.

> **"The handbook tells that Westlake Companeye have, for all these many centuries, left this place to itself,"** he said. *(...)* **"A great courtesy. I shall make every hallowed gesture which will - I know - satisfy the old guard employed in such a basement."**

> **"Hmm,"** said Igon. **"Hmm."**

> **"Aha, well met to you also, friend lock! So you have revealed your little secret to me then?"**

> **"How splendid. We are well-acquainted now."**

---

## Scene 2 — The Worm Locker

> **"What a courteous welcome,"** said Igon. *(...)* **"This department sets out the freshest batch for new faces, I see."**

> **"Ohh,"** he said, soft. **"Ohh, and what is this little singer?"**

> **"Hmm, might one ask what manner of figure is preferred?"** said Igon.

> **"Hah!"** Igon said. **"A fair request. Though I confess I do not recall the precise note."**

> **"And should I ask what is being measured, friend, or is that a discouraged inquiry?"**

> **"Ahh, little singer,"** said Igon. **"What a brief recital."**

---

## Scene 3 — The Four Server Rooms

> **"Ohh, what a lovely station,"** said Igon. **"And I do believe the song is nearer in this place. The oboe. I came here to find that sweet and gentle music, so that I may accompany it, not let it play alone in a dream until it dwindles."**

> **"Ahh. Then perhaps a different custom."**

> **"Friends, if battle it must be, I shall oblige."**

> **"Ohh- Ohh dear. I had not anticipated such an insular guard."**

> **"Ahh. Sealed against me. Hah."**

---

## Scene 4 — The Descent

> **"Ohh, elder corridor,"** he murmured into the iron, **"you are a vocal host. More welcome than I am accustomed to, I fear."**

---

## Scene 5 — The Conference Room

> **"Forgive this necessary violence,"** Igon panted.

> **"Ohh, friend below,"** Igon called, breath hitched between blows, **"such fluorescent light is very far off..."**

---

## Scene 6 — The Fax-Machine's Chamber

> *(No dialogue. Igon sits cross-legged in silence — his humming gone out "like a crushed diode" — and never answers Guilliam.)*
