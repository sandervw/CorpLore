# Rafe Scigley - Lore Consistency Review: Suggested Updates

## Scene 1

1. **"Ward of User Authentication"** - "Ward" is generic fantasy - replace with 'Salt-Magic of User Authentication'
2. **Carpet fraying missed** - Worn carpet "ground to indeterminacy" - add a few words noting exposed Warp.
3. **"Glyphboard" inconsistency** - Standardize to "glyphboard" throughout.

---

## Scene 3

1. **"January-class offense" conflates cult with penal code.** - replace with a term that does not blend cult identity and legal severity.
2. **Onboarding door hum is unattributed.** Suggest a Hold forming on a dead line.
3. **"Fiscal quarter he did not recognize"** - Strengthen with a specific anchor: a quarter predating the Year of Consolidation, or an expired god's season.

---

## Scene 4

1. **Missing Reading of the Frames.** Onboarding requires new employees to read each poster Word aloud with a senior member. The Rites of Integration should reference or subvert this.
2. **Steel dining trays need weapon justification.** Lore requires a justification, however nonsensical, for any weapon. One sentence of absurd rationale ("for catering purposes") is sufficient.
3. **Elevator folklore missed.** Rafe's date-confusion (13th/14th/15th) could be flavored by the temporal-severance belief about elevators.

---

## Scene 5

1. **Reading of the Frames ritual absent.** The Coordinator should invoke it, or Rafe should note its absence as a sign of the annex's degradation.
2. **Weapons absent.** Demons carry weapons openly. The confiscation of Rafe's root hook should make the group's disarmed state notable or humiliating.

---

## Scene 6

1. **No motivational posters.** Even a decommissioned room has posters per lore. A bare wall is cosmologically suspect and should be noted.
2. **"Bathroom tissue" missed.** Add a modifier: "marsh-pulped" or "swamp-bleached" tissue to invoke restroom-as-swamp lore.
3. **"Sport coat" repeated.** Add a mythic modifier on first use: "ash-colored sport coat" or "threadbare sport coat."

---

## Scene 7

1. **Death unnamed.** The she-demon's killing never uses "abrupt termination" or "passing away." The Coordinator's clipboard note or dialogue should include it.
2. **No motivational poster in anteroom.** A sealed surplus room is prime poster territory. Its Word (e.g., INITIATIVE) could foreshadow the trap.
3. **Iron keycard could be a sorcerous artifact.** One line acknowledging its electric-magic lineage would deepen the scene.

---

## Scene 10

1. **"Company-defined Words of Mighty Danger"** - Companies do not author the Words. Reframe as "sanctioned Words of Mighty Danger."
2. **January Financials reference is a strong hit.** Add a sensory detail from their lore at the collection notice moment: toner smell, the hunger, or an unfillable filing cabinet.
3. **Carpet/elevator texture absent at exit.** A brief elevator stillness beat when leaving the Hiring Annex would add minimal-cost world texture.
