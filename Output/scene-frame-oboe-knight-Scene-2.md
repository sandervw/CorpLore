# Scene Frame — oboe-knight-Scene-2

## Scene Parameters

**Senses:** taste, proprioception, touch
Taste grounds the desiccated-muffin moment; proprioception carries Igon's footwork and combat geometry; touch handles the cold plexiglass and the vibration of the speaker's note through the glass.

**Rhythms:** run-on, balanced
Run-on suits Igon's expansive, curious surveying; balanced pairs with the combat's call-and-response of blow and counter-blow.

**Scene shape:** accumulation
Pressure builds — treat tables, then the locker, then the note, then the wake, then the fight — each beat loading onto the last.

**Color palette:** rust-and-verdigris
Blue-bonefire ambient light plus oxidized metal and old bone surfaces. Keep warm tones out; this is a sealed, airless, greenish dark.

## Source Imprint

**Sentence-length distribution:** Alternates long descriptive sweeps (20–40 words) with short declarative punches (4–8 words). No comfortable middle range.

**Clause stacking:** Right-branching, additive ("and … and … and"), with occasional parenthetical asides mid-clause. Periodic sentences rare but used for payoff lines.

**Register:** Plain-to-archaic blend. No contractions in narration. Dialogue slightly more clipped. Latinate vocabulary used selectively for elevation, not habitually.

**Dominant sensory channels:** Visual (color, light, movement) primary; kinetic/action beats precise and economical.

**Signature tics:** Color named twice (shift mid-passage); action described in exact physical terms (what the body does, not how it feels emotionally); dialogue follows action immediately with no attribution delay.

**Do:** Name colors and light qualities directly. Follow action beats with short declarative sentences. Let dialogue land without interior reaction.
**Don't:** Pile internal emotional gloss onto physical events. Use adverbs to carry tone. Let a paragraph settle before something moves.

## Speech Rules

### Igon (Radiant Optimist)

- **Positive mode:** Warm exclamatory opener ("Ohh," / "Aha!") + greeting; self-deprecating "Hah!" after grandiosity or awkwardness.
- **Register:** Slightly formal, old-fashioned, courtly-but-conversational. Sparing contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 20 words. Short exclamations and ellipsis-trailed fragments allowed at emotional peaks.
- **Signature construction:** Requests framed as invitations / rhetorical questions.
- **Default-banned:** Commands or imperatives; modern slang; cynicism; mockery aimed outward.

### Guilliam (Analytic Machine-Voice)

- **Positive mode:** Interrupts itself mid-sentence and restarts on a different track; answers questions with warnings or refusals rather than information. Cap: fires on ~50% of Guilliam's lines.
- **Register:** Technical, jargon-heavy, formal-modern. No contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 25 words. Ends lines on a noun wherever possible.
- **Signature construction:** Begins most lines with a conjunction (And / But / Or). Cap: ~50% of lines.
- **Default-banned:** Warm or emotive language; "what does it feel like"-type questions; fragment-as-style.

---

## Forbidden Patterns
- No extended metaphors longer than one sentence
- No metaphors built on weather or seasons
- No isolated fragment sentences
- No description of breath as visible vapor
- No glowbug, lantern, or torchlight throwing shadows imagery
- No abstract nouns standing in for sensory description
- Do not name the bluetooth speaker's function or hint at its animating role
- Do not let Igon identify the played note as his own song
- Do not give Guilliam's voice warmth, hesitation, or any register that reads as personhood rather than process
- Do not describe combat wounds in terms of pain or sensation felt by the corpse — name only bone, joint, and trajectory
