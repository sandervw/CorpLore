# Igon — Dialogue Extract

*The whole of the lines pronounced by Igon (“the Oboe Knight”) of Allsode, accompanied by their marks of attribution, drawn from the scenes of the Oboe Knight. He speaks aloud through his iron helm, with a joviality that nothing intimidates, until the ultimate hall — where he says no more.*

---

## Scene 1 — The Entry into the Basement

> **“Oh, what a ravishing corridor of former times,”** says he into the iron of his helm.

> **“The manual reports that the Westlake Company has, throughout all these centuries, left this place to itself,”** says he. *(...)* **“What lofty courtesy. I shall accomplish each one of the consecrated gestures that will know how — of this I am certain — to content the ancient guard with which such a basement honours itself.”**

> **“Hmm,”** did Igon make. **“Hmm.”**

> **“Ah, to thee too the good day, friend latch! So here it is that thou confidest to me thy little secret?”**

> **“What splendour. Henceforth, we know each other well, thou and I.”**

---

## Scene 2 — The Locker of the Worm

> **“What a welcome full of courtesy,”** says Igon. *(...)* **“This department reserves the freshest batch for the new faces, from what I see.”**

> **“Oh,”** says he, in a soft voice. **“Oh, and what then is this little cantor?”**

> **“Hmm, would one dare to inquire after the figure that one prefers?”** says Igon.

> **“Ha!”** says Igon. **“There is a most legitimate request. Even though I avow it: the exact note escapes me.”**

> **“And must I ask what it is that one measures, my friend, or rather is that there a question that one discourages?”**

> **“Ah, little cantor,”** says Igon. **“What a brief recital that was.”**

---

## Scene 3 — The Four Halls of the Servers

> **“Oh, what a ravishing station,”** says Igon. **“And I do believe that the song makes itself nearer in this place. The oboe. It is to find this tender and soft music that I am come, in order to accompany it, and not to leave it to play alone in the hollow of a dream until it extinguishes itself.”**

> **“Ah. Then perhaps another custom.”**

> **“My friends, if it must come to arms, I shall lend myself to it willingly.”**

> **“Oh- Oh, my God. I did not at all expect a guard so reclusive.”**

> **“Ah. Closed against me. Ha.”**

---

## Scene 4 — The Descent

> **“Oh, venerable corridor,”** murmured he into the iron, **“thou art a most loquacious host. More welcoming than those to whom I am accustomed, this I fear.”**

---

## Scene 5 — The Conference Room

> **“Pardon me this violence that it was necessary,”** panted Igon.

> **“Oh, friend from below,”** flung Igon, the breath broken between the blows, **“such fluorescent light holds itself well far from here...”**

---

## Scene 6 — The Chamber of the Fax Machine

> *(No word. Igon holds himself seated cross-legged in the silence — his humming extinguished “such as a crushed diode” — and never does he answer Guilliam.)*
