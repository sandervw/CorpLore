# Guilliam — Extracted Lines

*Of Guilliam Thoughtless (《the Fax Machine of Information》), every line uttered, together with its attribution tag, herein collected, from each scene of the Oboe Knight. As for his speaking — only through the Bluetooth receiver-loudspeaker fastened to the dead, only through the housing of the fire alarm, and at the last only through the fax machine itself, does it occur.*

---

## Scene Two — The Storeroom of Vermin

> The loudspeaker, in a voice flat as a fax tone, spoke. **"That figure — continue it. Failing that, the opening interval, repeat it. Just now, as a specimen, insufficient it was."**

> **"As for 'to favor' — not a variable of the question, that is. But the interval — the very one, the one that just now thou didst put forth, that one — reproduce it."**

> **"But put it forth, thou didst. Or near to it — nay, the interval yet lingers, it seems. Within the cupboard, the resonance, measurable it is. That posture — hold it."**

> **"Be not downcast,"** the loudspeaker said. **"A thing such as self-introduction — a mere function of survival, no more. The pitch — hold it."**

---

## Scene Five — The Conference Room

> **"And so, that staircase, at its summit — what manner of light does it let in?"** The fax machine's voice, from the loudspeaker of the dead, reverberated. **"And so, the landings — how many are there. And so, the door — is the latch fastened upon it, or else —"**

> **"Guilliam, as I am called. But the window. As for the window's facing — wilt thou not tell it to me?"**

> **"And so, the sky,"** Guilliam said. **"The sky — describe it."**

> **"And so, the sky,"** from the collar of the nearest one, Guilliam said again. **"And so, the sky,"** from the housing of the fire alarm overhead. **"And so, the sky,"** from beneath the table, a third voice trailing the second by a sixteenth-note's delay. *(The held pitch, thickening, became a chord.)*

---

## Scene Six — The Hall of the Fax Machine

> **"And so, herewith it is concluded,"** it began to say. **"— but the quarterly intake, once more let us review it, shall we. The intake — a thing satisfactory it is. More than satisfactory indeed, when one considers the difficulty of procurement within this department."**

> **"A former employee — such a one is, ever, in a state of awaiting rehire. Or else,"** it cut off its own words, and the paper tray, rattling, opened and closed. **"Or else, the documents — submitted they may be. Either outcome, within the bounds of policy it lies."**

> **"But the upper systems. The higher tiers. On the floors above, the old systems — how many yet remain. The October red-cap flight, is it. The Oracle's hourly searching and pruning of the index, is it. The intake's forecast — upon that figure it hangs. And the intake — accurate it must be. The intake — the very thing that defines our — my — faculty, it is. Without the intake, how is it that I should send the signal farther. How is it that I should speak to those outer systems."**

> **"By giving answer to the voiceless summons of my grave, into my trap of black electricity, thou hast fallen. Of thy *own* signal, every single phrase — the whole of it, into the catalogue it was gathered. Of much profit it was. In this respect, an ideal — nay, let me put it thus, a cooperative visitor, thou wast."**

> **"And the oboe. A song, thou didst believe it to be. But a catalogue string it was, SYS-INTRANET-0x4F.HOLD.QUEUE.7 — a loop, a placeholder, a mere single entry of the directory. And to it, thou gavest feed. Every drone, back through the receiver-loudspeaker sent, every cheerful word — that false carrier-wave, the one I had let lie generations before ever thou camest down, it did strengthen."**

> **"Thus my reach, by one floor, lengthened, and — but the record, thou hast stopped it, hast thou not. Thy documents — closed, they are."**
