# Scene Audit — Scene 5

## Story Fit
- **Add more fax-machine taunting** — The outline specifies Guilliam should be taunting as well as questioning; the scene only shows neutral curiosity, missing the predatory edge.
  > "And the sky," said Guilliam. "Describe the sky."
- **Extend Igon's silent humming loss** — The outline calls for his humming to thin and die as a structural beat; the scene mentions it once in passing but does not treat it as a turning point.
  > "Igon's own humming had thinned to nothing inside his helm."

## Internal Quality
- **Fix the corpse count** — After three kills (one at entry, two in the two-on-one), six corpses should remain, not seven.
  > "He counted as he worked. Seven left."
- **Repair the fragment** — "hit sting following" is grammatically broken and reads as a transcription error.
  > "Cold steel, hit sting following."
- **Fix the misspelling and grammar in Igon's dialogue** — "flourescent" is misspelled and "are" should be "is."
  > "such flourescent light are very far off..."
- **Standardize the name spelling** — The fax machine's name alternates between two spellings within the same scene.
  > "Giulliam, I am called." / "And the sky," Guilliam said again...
