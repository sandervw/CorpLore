# Scene Frame — Scene 3

## Scene Parameters

**Senses:** kinaesthetic / touch / proprioception
The extended combat sequence demands body-in-space awareness over sight or sound; Igon's cuts and pivots should register as felt weight and muscle, not watched performance.

**Rhythms:** periodic / run-on
Periodic sentences for the slow opening and the prayer failures (tension building to delayed resolution); run-on during combat surges when momentum overtakes grammar.

**Scene shape:** reversal
The fight arc maps directly: Igon presses, is pressed, nearly breaks, then turns it. One structural reversal, not a steady climb.

**Color palette:** sea-glass-and-bleach
Pale greens, washed-out whites, faint aqua from the server-glow and the golden water cooler against bone-grey stucco. Nothing saturated.

---

## Source Imprint

**Sentence-length distribution:** Very short sentences (3-6 words) alternate with medium-long ones (15-30 words); almost no mid-range. Fragments used for punctuation, not atmosphere.

**Clause stacking:** Right-branching; actions trail off into subordinate detail. Parenthetical asides inserted without ceremony ("of this I am certain").

**Register:** Plain, slightly archaic diction. No contractions in formal speech; contractions appear only in quick back-and-forth. Latinate words absent.

**Dominant sensory channel:** Tactile and kinetic — bodies fitting things, things falling heavily, water hissing.

**Signature tics:** Dialogue tags kept minimal ("said," "declared,"). Scene-break via single short sentence. Action advances via blunt declarative before any commentary.

**Do:** Lead with action; let objects have precise names; use the blunt declarative as a scene pivot. **Don't:** Linger in interiority; reach for figurative language; let a sentence call attention to its own construction.

---

## Speech Rules

### Igon

- **Positive mode:** Warm exclamatory opener ("Ohh," / "Aha!") + greeting; self-deprecating "Hah!" after grandiosity or awkwardness.
- **Register:** Slightly formal, old-fashioned, courtly-but-conversational. Sparing contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 20 words. Short exclamations and ellipsis-trailed fragments allowed at emotional peaks.
- **Signature construction:** Requests framed as invitations / rhetorical questions.
- **Default-banned:** Commands or imperatives; modern slang; cynicism; mockery aimed outward.

---

## Forbidden Patterns
- No more than one simile per paragraph.
- No metaphors built on weather or seasons.
- No parallel-clause balanced sentences ("he did X. she did Y.").
- No colons used to introduce clauses.
- No eyes described as reflective surfaces (mirrors, water, glass).
- No silence described as a physical weight.
- No sententious closing lines (one-line moralisms).
- Do not name or editorialize the Bluetooth-necromancy mechanic.
- Do not have Igon consciously connect the synthetic notes to his oboe song.
- Do not describe the combat as one-sided or heroic in tone.
- Do not let the cursed-carpet beat read as comedic; render it as a piece of ancient, indifferent mechanism.
