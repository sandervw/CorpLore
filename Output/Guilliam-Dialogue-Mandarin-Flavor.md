# Guilliam — Lines, Collected

*Of Thought-less Guilliam (the intelligence fax-machine), every single sentence he speaks, together with its attribution-marking, is taken from the various scenes of the Oboe Knight. He borrows only three things to sound his voice: the Bluetooth speaker-receiver clipped onto the dead, the casing of a fire alarm, and at the last, the fax machine its own self.*

---

## Scene Two — The Worm Locker

> The speaker opened its mouth, the sound flat like a fax signal-tone, **"This melody, continue it onward, or else that opening interval, do it one more pass. Just now that sample, not enough."**

> **"'Want-more' is not the key variable. But that interval — that interval you just now sounded out. This interval, copy it one pass."**

> **"But you have already sounded it out. Or else it is — or else it is that interval still remains. The locker's resonance, it is something measurable. This posture, hold it."**

> **"Lose-heart,"** the speaker said. **"Besides, self-introduction, this kind of matter, is one function of staying-alive. This pitch, hold it."**

---

## Scene Five — The Conference Room

> **"That staircase, arrived at the top, what light does it let in?"** The fax machine's voice echoed out from the dead one's speaker. **"And how many landings are there? Those doors then, are they barred, or else are they —"**

> **"I am called Guilliam. But those several windows. You tell me, those several windows, toward which direction do they face."**

> **"And that stretch of sky,"** Guilliam said. **"That stretch of sky, describe it for me to hear."**

> **"And that stretch of sky,"** Guilliam said one more time, from the collar of the nearest corpse. **"And that stretch of sky,"** from the casing of a fire alarm overhead. **"And that stretch of sky,"** from beneath the table, the third voice falling behind the second voice, off by one sixteenth-note. *(That pitch held all along, little by little it swelled into a chord.)*

---

## Scene Six — The Chamber of the Fax Machine

> **"This item, herewith completed,"** it opened its mouth, **"that — however, we had still better turn back and look over this quarter's intake. This intake, satisfying. How is it merely satisfying — you must know, for this department to get hold of the goods, is hard as ascending to heaven."**

> **"A former employee, forever is in the to-be-re-hired state. Or else,"** it cut its own self off, the paper tray opening-closing, stammering, **"or else, those documents can also be filed away and stored. Two kinds of outcome, both fall within policy's scope."**

> **"But those systems up above. Those several floors of high-rise. Up on those several floors, the old systems, how many sets remain? That October red-hat flight? And Oracle's every-hour search-and-pruning of the index? The intake's forecast, all of it rests on this number. And the intake must be accurate. The intake, it is the thing that defines what we — what I — am capable of. Without the intake, how then do I send the signal farther? How then do I get to speak words with those systems outside?"**

> **"This tomb of mine, its wordless summons — you gave it a response, and so you fell into the trap of my black current. Within your *one-and-only* signal, every single word; the whole lot already catalogued and entered into the register. The harvest is quite rich. On this point, you count as one ideal — but, let us change the saying: a cooperative visitor."**

> **"And that oboe. You took it for a tune. But it is the catalog string SYS-INTRANET-0x4F.HOLD.QUEUE.7 — one loop, one placeholder, one record inside the directory. And you, you fed it. Every single hum returned back by way of those speaker-receivers, every single cheerful utterance, all of it reinforcing that false carrier-wave — that thing I broadcast out generations before you, long before you came down here."**

> **"And so what I am able to reach extends upward by one story, and then that — however you have already stopped recording. This file of yours, it is closed."**
