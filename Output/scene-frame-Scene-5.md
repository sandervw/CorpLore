# Scene Frame — Scene-5

## Scene Parameters

**Senses:** balance, smell, sight
Balance anchors every exchange — Igon losing and recovering footing tracks the battle's arc physically. Smell marks kills and crowd. Sight governs the silver suits and the advancing dead, kept precise and inventory-sharp.

**Rhythms:** run-on / paratactic
Run-on during the press of combat; paratactic as Igon steps back, counts dead, and the music swells. The rhythm shifts signal the mini-arc of exhaustion.

**Scene shape:** accumulation
Each corpse felled adds another note to the hold music; each addition feeds Guilliam further; the scene gains mass toward a bursting-full silence at the last blow.

**Color palette:** white-and-ebony
Silver business-formal suits; the ebony table splits the room into two tones; Igon's pale knuckles, the white-light of embedded speakers going dark.

## Source Imprint

- Sentence length alternates sharply: 3-8 word declarations ("Silence returned to the work-room.") beside 30-45 word rolling sentences. Almost no mid-range.
- Right-branching clause chains, often trailing into a participial or prepositional tail after the main verb.
- Register is formal-archaic with occasional bathetic deflation ("with only fair success, so that he fell heavily from time to time, to wallow and roll").
- Dialogue tags are almost always plain said/declared/cried; no adverbial modification.
- Repetition of a proper noun as a sentence-opener to resume after a pause ("Sadlark lurched forward." / "Sadlark came to a halt").
- Action reported in brisk declaratives, then immediately followed by one longer clause of consequence.
- No metaphor in action passages; direct, unadorned reporting of physical event.

**Do:** alternate short declarative sentences with long right-branching ones; name things plainly; use repetition of a subject noun to resume action after a break; follow each action with one consequence clause.
**Don't:** stack metaphors in action; use adverbial dialogue tags; hover in mid-range sentence lengths; editorialize from the narrator's perspective.

## Speech Rules

### Igon

- **Positive mode:** Warm exclamatory opener ("Ohh," / "Aha!") + greeting; self-deprecating "Hah!" after grandiosity or awkwardness.
- **Register:** Slightly formal, old-fashioned, courtly-but-conversational. Sparing contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 20 words. Short exclamations and ellipsis-trailed fragments allowed at emotional peaks.
- **Signature construction:** Requests framed as invitations / rhetorical questions.
- **Default-banned:** Commands or imperatives; modern slang; cynicism; mockery aimed outward.

### Guilliam

- **Positive mode:** Interrupts itself mid-sentence and restarts on a different track; answers questions with warnings or refusals rather than information. Cap: fires on ~50% of Guilliam's lines.
- **Register:** Technical, jargon-heavy, formal-modern. No contractions.
- **Sentence-length cap:** 25 words. Ends lines on a noun wherever possible.
- **Signature construction:** Begins most lines with a conjunction (And / But / Or). Cap: ~50% of lines.
- **Default-banned:** Warm or emotive language; "what does it feel like"-type questions; fragment-as-style.

---

## Forbidden Patterns

- no extended metaphors longer than one sentence
- no similes whose vehicle is a household item
- no consecutive sentences of the same length
- no comma splices
- no silence described as a physical weight
- no eyes described as reflective surfaces (mirrors, water, glass)
- no abstract nouns standing in for sensory description
- No lingering interiority mid-combat: Igon must not pause to reflect
- No speaker-destruction described identically twice: vary the means and the bodily aftermath each time
- No false-hold-music described as "growing louder" more than once in plain paraphrase
- No Guilliam line that directly announces its purpose
