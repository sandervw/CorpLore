# Igon — The Gathered-Out Dialogue

*Of Igon of Allsode ("the Oboe-Knight") his every spoken-out line, together with their belonging-to naming-labels, lifted out from the Oboe-Knight scenes. Through his iron-helm he speaks loudly, merrily and un-frighten-ably, all the way down to the very-last chamber — where already nothing does he say.*

---

## First Scene — The Stepping-In to the Underfloor

> **"Oooh, what a beauteous, agèd corridor-here!"** — into the iron of his helm did he speak.

> **"The hand-book it tells in tales, that the Westlake Companeye through these many-many centuries left this place unto-itself,"** — said he. *(...)* **"A right great courteousness. Every hallowèd movement shall I make, the which — well do I know it — satisfies the agèd-old guard, the which unto such an underfloor was hired-into-service."**

> **"Hm,"** — said Igon. **"Hm."**

> **"Aha, a good meeting unto thee also, lock-friend! So then, hast thou un-covered before me thy little secret?"**

> **"How splendid. From-now-on well do we know one-another."**

---

## Second Scene — The Maggot-Cupboard

> **"What a courteous receiving-in,"** — said Igon. *(...)* **"This department the freshest portion spreads-out unto the new faces, so do I see it."**

> **"Oooh,"** — said he softly. **"Oooh, and of-what-sort of little song-singer might this one here be?"**

> **"Hm, might-one ask-it, of-what-sort of figure would-be-liked here?"** — said Igon.

> **"Hah!"** — said Igon. **"A rightful asking. Though I confess-it, onto the exact voice already do I not remember."**

> **"And shall-I-ask-it, my friend, that what-indeed do they measure here, or is this perhaps an un-wished-for prying?"**

> **"Ahh, little song-singer,"** — said Igon. **"What a brief little performance was this-one."**

---

## Third Scene — The Four Server-Rooms

> **"Oooh, what a beauteous station,"** — said Igon. **"And so do I believe, nearer sounds the song in this-place-here. The oboe. For-this did I come hither, that I might find-it, this sweet and gentle music, that I might accompany it, and not let it sound all-by-its-one-self in a dream, until it does fade-away."**

> **"Ahh. So then perhaps an other-sort of custom."**

> **"My friends, if a fighting there must be, I do enough unto it."**

> **"Oooh — oooh, woe. Onto such a closed-up guard did I not count."**

> **"Ahh. Bolted-shut against me. Hah."**

---

## Fourth Scene — The Down-Descending

> **"Oooh, agèd corridor,"** — murmured he into the iron, **"thou indeed art a speech-full house-host. Much more cordial, than that whereunto, alas, I have grown-accustomed-myself."**

---

## Fifth Scene — The Negotiation-Hall

> **"Forgive ye this necessity-bound violence,"** — panted Igon.

> **"Oooh, my down-below friend,"** — cried-out Igon, his breath stop-and-stopping amid the strokes, **"that fluorescent-tubed brightness very-very far away is yet..."**

---

## Sixth Scene — The Chamber of the Fax-Machine

> *(Not a single word. Igon with crossed-laid legs, mutely sits — his humming gone-out, "as-if a smashed-asunder diode" —, and never does he answer unto Guilliam.)*
