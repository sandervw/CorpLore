# Koschei

## Scene 1 - The Empty Office

**Arrival:**

I climbed to two hundred and fifty third heaven, a heaven of mushrooms, of millet, of tillagers, of much eating, and of little bother, very early. A shine of dawn hugged Obelisk on its tall east face. I came to Warm's Route, however, and Warm's Route is over seven miles from dawn glimpsing, on Obelisk's west cheek, and both cheeks are bigly windowed, in thick, clear crystal: maybe one hundred big man's feet tall these windows are, and such awesome windows are glass on a dead bulb, in west dawn's umbra.

Of a mind to lay eyes on Perli, a she-demon I well know, I came: Perli is a tillager of two hundred and fifty third heaven, which does not make her alike to the usual lambs and ewes that are most tillagers, or any demon who lives in a safe upper heaven, say, any heaven above one hundred and fifty. A worn carpet leads to Perli. Down Warm's Route it goes all the way, from a timeless elevator, to a marble coffee altar, where a worship of demons prays to a chalice of ancient grounds. I came down this carpet road, skirted the tillagers' dawn rite - they are jesters, giving each dawn a 'free tax' of their mushrooms to their floor's Mulcher department, much of a folly! - and knocked, a tall window's grey and dry darklight against my spine, on the door of Perli's cube.

I learned she was gone.

---

**Discovery:**

*In Arrears.*

Two words of a crayon of indigo color only were they, which curved in learned letters on that crystal window across from Perli's cube - but though I, had read only lightly the books and papers of occult, still I turned my sight from such written wax. Hard were they of reading anyway, at that hour, words of a blue tint against dawn of a blue tint, and even so had I seen them, and even had I not, surely the stink in that air outside of Perli's cube - of fresh mown field grass, with something of dung mingling - must have gossiped to my brain, and said to it: "These are Words of a Mighty Danger." Nigromancy.

"Ostrabawgewlus," said I, with furrowed down-looking, and laving my chin with a broad hand, "might it well be that you have a far reaching ear. Your pen's shape is a secret from the knowing of a modern lay-demon, one unstudied in the lords of Obelisk past: I am studied. I have read *Vampires of a White Pinhole sky*, your opus you claimed in its introduction, and of a truth, for being 175 years old, that treatise still defines modern astrology. Well, I might guess rightly your signiture, and might you hear me, and might it be you took Perli: know you then that I, Gneiss Rong of a soubriquet 'Big', shall blast you to ashes, no matter-"

**Parley:**

"Fool," said a voice. "You should keep that name off of your tongue, or would you with wareless threatening bring that old demon spell lord's pain down on all of our skulls?"
