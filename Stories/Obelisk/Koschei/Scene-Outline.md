# Koschei — Scene Breakdown (~10,250 w)

## Scene Types

- **Sanctum** — home-base craft/preparation
- **Parley** — bargaining, interrogation, exposition delivered through dialogue
- **Journey** — travel and arrival
- **Wonder** — spectacle, strange-place discovery
- **Violence** — fights, kills, torture
- **Chase** — pursuit or flight
- **Coda** — resolution, justice, aftermath

## Scenes

### 1. The Empty Office — 850 — Parley

Gneiss visits Perli's office on floor 253, and finds her not within (Open with a bit of NEW worldbuilding - office description, specific name, purpose). An old Word of Mighty Danger, scrawled in bright indigo crayon on the window by her cubicle, has transferred Perli below floor 134, into the department of Ostrabawgewlus. Two officers from the local Mulch Department stand in the empty space, "reasoning" that it will probably work itself out (*subtext* - they don't want to suffer the same fate as the other two). They file the rescue as "pending." Gneiss, after arguing, tells them he'll go alone alone; they warn him of the prior two attempts.

**Arrival:** Floor 253 west, Cookie Boulevard: a department of tillage under hundred-foot crystal windows, twenty-eight tillager families in brick cubicles, a voluntary tax of millet and mushrooms rendered to the floor's Mulcher unit. Worn carpet runs between a timeless elevator and a marble coffee altar with its chalice cult. Gneiss mocks the ceremony aloud, then sights Perli's one-bed cubicle and her empty violet velvet chair.

**Discovery:** "In Arrears", indigo crayon on the crystal window opposite her cubicle. Gneiss withholds his sight, reads it sidelong; the spent Word smells of mown grass over skunk and dung. He knows the fine hand from a 175-year-old astrology treatise, *Vampires of the White Pinhole Sky*, and names Ostrabawgewlus aloud.

**Parley:** Mulcher deputies M. Brayve and M. Perpull hold the cubicle, Brayve with a hand over his eyes, debating how the indigo crayon was forged, a waxworks of replicas below. Their dread shows only in posture and hissed shushes, *subtext*.

**Argument:** Gneiss argues the Word reads as plunder; the deputies raise the rescue queue, Perli eleventh, forty quarters until attempted rescue. He declares he will go down himself. Brayve objects that no demon above 134 is an employee of the low halls; Perpull names the prior attempters, tillagers Daz'lyng (sight) and Owlisch (hearing), blamed on Red Apes. Seeds Scene 2. "He's takin' the long lunch."

**Personal:** A translucent figure fades into the window glass: Withazz, wraithwise, yellow greatcoat of thirteen buttons, under distress. A short vision: use the Coat of Seven Senses in the coming travail, conceal six senses from Ostrabawgewlus. One oblique nod to the night of full moon under sunset bush. He vanishes.

### 2. The Blind and the Deaf — 950 — Parley

Gneiss interviews the two coworkers who tried the descent to Ostrabawgewlus's floor before: one came home blind, one deaf. The deaf one is currently to help the blind one learn to use a braille keyboard, so that he can build his forever-world. Gneiss finally convinces them to pause and help him. Between the three of, them they cobble together a map to the location of Ostrabawgewlus's department, on floor 130. The path takes Gneiss through three sealed floors, the keycard scanners, and the guardians who open doors only for a sense. (Moment of humor here: the blind one can't draw the map, and the deaf one can't hear what Gneiss is saying - some kind of dialogue-only "who's-on-first"-type beat of humor.) They warn him about the first two guardians, but can give him no info on the third.Gneiss smiles (like in scene 1), confident that his coat and gatling axe will spare him from whatever he might encounter.

**Arrival:** Gneiss finds the pair in Daz'lyng's cubicle (NEW - where a blinded tillager lives now, invention slot). Owlisch guides Daz'lyng's fingers on a braille keyboard; onscreen, the forever-world (NEW - what a forever-world is, invention slot). Gneiss takes wry inventory of the damage before announcing himself. Wear: braille keyboard's home-row studs already rubbed shiny from fumbling drill. Particularity: forever-world so far, one brick cubicle and seventeen rows of perfect millet.

**Parley:** He names Perli and asks the way down. Both refuse, reciting the Red Apes story too loudly, near-rehearsed (*subtext* - the true price shames them). What turns them: Perli's past kindness, one concrete remembered favor apiece. Economic: Perli covered Owlisch's millet tax three harvests running, unasked. Quantification: both tellings number exactly five Red Apes, never varying, suspiciously identical.

**Mapmaking:** Dialogue-only who's-on-first: Daz'lyng dictates landmarks he cannot draw, Owlisch draws turns he cannot hear, Gneiss the sweating relay. Throwaway landmarks spent freely - Stairwell Grommet, the Xerox Chapel, Vending Row. The map converges on floor 130, three sealed doors between. Cultural: map drawn on the reverse of a Voluntary Taxation receipt, Form 11-B. Particularity: Daz'lyng's landmark, "the copier that says OW when it staples."

**Warnings:** The Furnace scanner took Daz'lyng's sight; the Storm floor took Owlisch's hearing. How Owlisch passed the first door unmaimed - invention slot (continuity: why sense-prices differ per visitor). Of the third floor, nothing; neither went past his loss. Both agree the scanners, not the guardians, do the taking. Sensory: Daz'lyng's last sight, the scanner light ticking from red to green. Agency: Owlisch swears the scanner bore a fresh inspection sticker, initialed, undated.

**Personal:** Gneiss gives thanks in his fashion and the scene 1 smile, patting coat and gatling axe. Before he reaches the corridor the lesson resumes behind him, key-clicks steady (*subtext* - what the descent leaves of a demon). His confidence reads thinner to the reader than to him. Malfunction: one braille key sticks; Owlisch thumps it free without looking up. Quantification: thirty-seven key-clicks follow Gneiss down the corridor before the sound fades.

### 3. Below 134 — 700 — Journey

Time-compressed descent through dead cubicle strata — wheevil-gnawed, Mr. Montag sirens far below. Gneiss spares the Moon with a Face, a talking mutant door, rather than burn through it; it creaks open a forgotten stairwell and asks to be remembered.

### 4. The Buzzing Furnace — 900 — Violence

Air thick as sludge, humid, packed with clouds of midges. The first guardian names its price — Gneiss's sense of smell. Gneiss kills it against its own scanner instead, then cuts the Frontschwein, a humanoid pig, free of the fly-glue pits.

### 5. Withazz of the Landing — 600 — Parley

On a mezzanine landing, Withazz — gold-cloaked sorcerous engineer, every utterance a questionnaire of choices — stands ringed by wheevils. Gneiss scatters them rather than slip past unseen. In payment, Withazz poses the paths ahead and marks which scanner cannot be cheated.

### 6. The Floor of Lightning Storms — 800 — Chase

Wind fists Gneiss on the chin; in the far distance a bass drum shakes the ceiling of his world. The second guardian demands his hearing. Gneiss refuses, runs the storm-rows, threads the door in a lightning-blink, and gathers up the storm-thrown Toad Gargoyle.

### 7. The Jungle of Eights — 1,000 — Violence

A dense forest of webs. The third guardian cannot be slain — every stroke lands; nothing dies. Exhausted, Gneiss pays: the keycard scanner draws out his secret seventh sense. The last door opens on the deep department.

### 8. Five Against Four — 1,600 — Violence

Exposed by mischance to the four servants of Ostrabawgewlus — the Vantablue, Odd Life, Lead, and Gamma Engineers — Gneiss fights one against four until the spared arrive: Frontschwein, Toad Gargoyle, the Moon with a Face, Withazz. Five against four; the Engineers are broken.

### 9. The Unsensed Ear — 800 — Wonder

Coatwise — unseen, unheard, untouchable — Gneiss shadows Ostrabawgewlus's first audience with Perli. The Master of the Thousand Replicas, tenderly, boasts a first piece of his deathlessness: something braided, somewhere low, past the last living floor.

### 10. What the Villain Tells His Guest — 900 — Parley

Two more shadowed encounters. To Perli, the villain speaks freely: a tea kettle inside a braided money tree; a music box inside the kettle; a Chocolate Porcupine at the heart. His tenderness curdles to cruelty; Gneiss, unsensed, holds still and completes the secret.

### 11. The Braided Money Tree — 700 — Chase

A race to the hiding place as suspicion wakes above. Gneiss unbraids the money tree, opens kettle and music box, and — any animal can eat a hedgehog if it dares — swallows the Chocolate Porcupine whole while servant-things close in.

### 12. Severance — 450 — Coda

Somewhere he cannot see, unwitnessed, Ostrabawgewlus comes undone mid-sentence. Gneiss walks Perli up toward the habitable floors. The seventh sense never returns; he tells his followers it was the one he used least.
