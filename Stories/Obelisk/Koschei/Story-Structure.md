# Koschei, Reframed - Story Structure

> A CorpLore/Obelisk reframe of *Koschei the Deathless*.

## Premise

The villain, **Kwaint** (the Hisoka/Arioch demon), sealed his soul into an ancient Obelisk Replicas artifact in an age long passed, and hid it. Every other demon employed by obelisk can die, or be fired. He cannot.

His hidden life nests in a replica inside a replica inside a vault, hidden not at the top but at the bottom, down past floor 134. Safest where the monsters are.

Now, Chaco has "kidnapped" Gneiss's brother, **Nimbol**. Using an old Word of Mighty Danger, he transferred Gneiss's brother by magic into his own department - below floor 134. Gneiss and his brother are opposites, but they secretly love each other.

## Structure/plot

**Setup** Two earlier demons, friends/coworkers of Nimbol, tried the descent and came home empty-handed. One was blind; the other, deaf.

**The Descent (three key scanners).** Gneiss undertakes a long descent below 134 (use time-compression in prose here). He encounters three security doors, each guarded by a magical, cursed being, each sealed by a magical keycard scanner, which the being will only open for a price - one of Gneiss's senses. Gneiss is able to avoid one or two of these (through violence), but ends up losing at least one sense to an unslayable guardian.

**The Unsensed Ear.** Petr hides under his mother's cloak across three visits to overhear the secret. **Gneiss doesn't hide. He is already unseen.** The Coat of the Seven Senses turns eavesdropping into his native mode. He shadows the villain through three encounters, invisible, collecting the deathless secret piece by piece while the villain speaks freely to someone he thinks is alone.

**4. The Spared Creatures.** Petr wins with a bear, otter, hawk, fish he'd spared. Reframe as **Creatures Electric Gneiss chose not to kill** on the way down:
- a Butler cat (nine lives, itself half-deathless, a mirror)
- a Mr. Montag whose siren shatters the vault glass
- wheevils that chew through a carapace-sealed door
- Mercy as the currency ordinary demons never spend.

**5. The Crushing.** Petr crushes the egg; Kastchey drops dead instantly. Gneiss reaches the nested artifact and unmakes it. The deathless man ends mid-sentence, mid-gesture, undone by a thing happening somewhere he can't see.
