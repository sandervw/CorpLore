# Koschei - Outline

> A CorpLore/Obelisk reframe of *Koschei the Deathless*. Past-tense first-person.

## Premise

The villain, **Ostrabawgewlus**, Master of the Thousand Replicas, (the Hisoka/Arioch demon), sealed his soul into an ancient replica Treat, in an age long passed, and hid it. Every other demon of Obelisk's heavens can eat the Long Lunch, or be cast down. He cannot.

His hidden life nests in a hidden replica: a Chocolate Porcupine, inside a music box, inside a tea kettle, inside a braided money tree, down in hell 130. Safest where the monsters are.

Now, Ostrabawgewlus has "kidnapped" Gneiss's first love, **Sirrel**. Using old Words of Mighty Danger, he transferred Sirrel by magic into his own warren - hell 130.

## Structure/plot

**Setup** Two earlier demons, Myoot and Kallus, fellow tillagers of Sirrel, tried the descent and came home empty-handed. One was blind; the other, deaf.

**The Descent (three gates).** Gneiss undertakes a long descent down the heavens (use time-compression in prose here). He comes to three gates on three hells, each held by a cursed ogre, who will open its passage only for a price - one of Gneiss's senses. Gneiss is able to avoid one or two of these (through violence), but ends up losing at least one sense (his secret seventh) to an unslayable guardian.
- Hell 133: The Buzzing Furnace - the air is like sludge, thick and humid, packed with clouds of midges and flies
- Hell 132: The Floor of Lightning Storms - the wind rises, blasts Gneiss on the chin; in the far distance, a bass drum shakes the ceiling of his world
- Hell 131: The Jungle of Eights - a dense forest of webs

**Spared Mutants.** On the way down, Gneiss chose to spare four unusual-allies. They come to his aid when he is unintentionally exposed to the servants of Ostrabawgewlus - the Vantablue, Odd Life, Lead, and Gamma Engineers.
- Do a huge, 1v4 then 5v4 battle
- The four servants of Ostrabawgewlus - The Vantablue, Odd Life, Lead, and Gamma Engineers
- The four allies: The Frontschwein (new creature electric - humanoid pig), The Toad Gargoyle (new creature electric) The Moon with a Face (a talking mutant door, painted/shaped like a crescent moon), Withazz

**The Unsensed Ear.** After he finds Ostrabawgewlus, Gneiss's Coat of the Seven Senses gives him the edge he needs. He shadows the villain through three encounters, invisible, unhearable, unable-to-be-touched/felt; collecting the deathless secret piece by piece while the villain speaks freely to Sirrel.

**Any Animal Can Eat a Hedgehog...** Gneiss reaches the soul inside the hedgehog; he swallows it. The deathless demon is undone by a thing happening somewhere he can't see. Gneiss recovers his love, but his seventh sense has been permanently stolen (seed for a larger narrative to tie together the stories).
