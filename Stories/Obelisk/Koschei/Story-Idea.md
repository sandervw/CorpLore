# Koschei - Outline

> A CorpLore/Obelisk reframe of *Koschei the Deathless*.

## Premise

The villain, **Ostrabawgewlus**, Master of the Thousand Replicas, (the Hisoka/Arioch demon), sealed his soul into an ancient Obelisk Replicas Treat, in an age long passed, and hid it. Every other demon employed by obelisk can die, or be fired. He cannot.

His hidden life nests in an hidden Obelisk replica: a Chocolate Porcupine, inside a music box, inside a tea kettle, inside a braided money tree, down past floor 134. Safest where the monsters are.

Now, Ostrabawgewlus has "kidnapped" Gneiss's first love, **Perli**. Using an old Word of Mighty Danger, he transferred Perli by magic into his own department - below floor 134.

## Structure/plot

**Setup** Two earlier demons, friends/coworkers of Perli, tried the descent and came home empty-handed. One was blind; the other, deaf.

**The Descent (three key scanners).** Gneiss undertakes a long descent below 134 (use time-compression in prose here). He encounters three security doors on three special floors, each guarded by a magical, cursed being, each sealed by a magical keycard scanner, which the being will only open for a price - one of Gneiss's senses. Gneiss is able to avoid one or two of these (through violence), but ends up losing at least one sense (his secret seventh) to an unslayable guardian.
- Floor 1: The buzzing Furnace - the air is like sludge, thick an humid, packed with clouds of midges and flies
- Floor 2: The Floor of Lightning Storms - the wind rises, blasts Geniss on the chin; in the far distance, a bass drum shakes the ceiling of his world
- Floor 3: The Jungle of Eights - a dense forest of webs

**Spared Mutants.** On the way down, Gneiss chose to spare four unusual-allies. They come to his aid when he is unintentionally exposed to the servants of Ostrabawgewlus - the Vantablue, Odd Life, Lead, and Gamma Engineers.
- Do a huge, 1v4 then 5v4 battle
- The four servants of Ostrabawgewlus - The Vantablue, Odd Life, Lead, and Gamma Engineers
- The four allies: The Frontschwein (new creature electric - humanoid pig), The Toad Gargoyle (new creature electric) The Moon with a Face (a talking mutant door, painted/shaped like a crescent moon), Withazz

**The Unsensed Ear.** After he finds Ostrabawgewlus, Gneiss's Coat of the Seven Senses gives him the edge he needs. He shadows the villain through three encounters, invisible, unhearable, unable-to-be-touched/felt; collecting the deathless secret piece by piece while the villain speaks freely to Perli.

**Any Animal Can Eat a Hedgehog...** Gneiss reaches the soul inside the hedgehog; he swallows it. The deathless demon is undone by a thing happening somewhere he can't see. Gneiss recovers his love, but his seventh sense has been permanently stolen (seed for a larger narrative to tie together the stories).
