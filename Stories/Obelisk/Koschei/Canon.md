# Koschei - Canon

## Chapter 1 - Of A Crayon

- Sirrel: tillager she-demon of heaven 253, Warm's Route, west cheek. Tillagers give a dawn "free tax" of mushrooms to their floor's Mulcher department. Floor speak: "fifty three" = 253, hells = lowest 133 of Obelisk's 1,056 planes.
- Word: "In Arrears", indigo crayon on crystal across from her cube, a stink of mown grass and dung. Gneiss knows Ostrabawgewlus's hand of *Vampires of a White Pinhole Sky*, his 175-year-old treatise. Spoken, that name courts his far ear: mulchers hiss "ZZZZZ".
- Mulchers M. Brayve and M. Laym: Sirrel filed eleventh (Sower, Mamuth ahead), forty-quarter limit, "He'll eat that Long Lunch." Prior seekers: Myoot blinded, Kallus deafened, Red Apes blamed.
- Withazz: window wraith, wool coat of honeybee yellow. His Seeming: jewel of an oyster, night of round moon, bush of heavy tomatoes. Pass three doors, find four friends, fight four foes, hide from six of seven.

## Chapter 2 - Of A Map

- Myoot (blind) and Kallus (deaf, hands burned) live in a rusted courtyard cube, once borough storage, resettled by Boss Hoalsail. Plecent: wife of Myoot.
- Forever-world: a game on a looking lens. Myoot drills a secondhand braille letterboard, 's' steps back.
- Map, drawn by Gneiss on a free tax scroll 11-B: L-shaped road, Surrance Consortium, timeless Lift down nineteen heavens, Sempraland's wall, salt-pentacle sleep law, memory machine that only says 'ow', Vendingrow, sea beast of Oikodespotes Lake, goat land, bathroom marsh.
- Ostrabawgewlus lairs at hell 130, of Wayger telling (Waygers: nomad hunter demons of low lands). Two ogres, gates of hells 133 and 132, each tricks a sense to open: Myoot paid eyes, Kallus ears. Hells hold Red Apes, Mr. Montag, Creatures Electric.
- Gneiss bears a Gatling Laser Axe: crescent head of auld metal, neon glow, six pommel tubes. His Coat of three buttons hides him unsensed.

## Chapter 3 - Of A Way I Did Go

- Route: L-corner from Myoot's cube, 20 miles through Surrance Consortium, then a timeless Lift (no time passes outside; days pass within, five songs looping).
- Hoortulon: financial lord, caravan by goatback (goat Edji) over the Steam Bog of Canne to Ives on Stall. Son Femik: nightly peekaboo, "Seeyou!" - lose and be eaten.
- Credleburg, heaven 212: Feast Day of Saint Mantyck, who melted a foe army (Brotherhood of Black Swords) by focusing sun-rays. Gneiss leaves at dawn, Sirrel goads him on.
- Heaven 160, then "A Belt Debatable": three heavens without sun/moon-tubes, wickerdemon guides sold for credit stamps, sound carries oddly in the dark. Ends at heaven 134, arrested.
