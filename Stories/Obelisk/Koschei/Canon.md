# Koschei - Canon

## Scene 1 - The Empty Office

- Perli: tillager, floor 253 west, Cookie Boulevard, the sole one-bed brick cubicle, violet velvet chair; her department of 28 pays a voluntary millet-and-mushroom tax to the floor Mulcher unit.
- The Word: "In Arrears", indigo crayon on the crystal window; spent-Word residue smells of mown grass over skunk and dung; Gneiss knows Ostrabawgewlus's neat hand from a 175-year-old astrology treatise, *Vampires of the White Pinhole Sky*.
- Mulchers M. Brayve and M. Perpull: rescue filed eleventh in queue, "within 40 quarters"; "taking the long lunch" = going below 134; prior attempters were tillagers Daz'lyng (blinded) and Owlisch (deafened), maimings blamed on Red Apes.
- Withazz contacts Gneiss as a window apparition (yellow greatcoat, thirteen buttons); bids him use the Coat and conceal six senses from Ostrabawgewlus; the seventh goes unmentioned.
