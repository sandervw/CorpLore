# Rafe Scigley: Scene 10 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

Rafe emerges from the Onboarding Annex into a deep HR processing office where the Aychar awaits. Believing his survival without a signed contract constitutes failure, Rafe expects to be released. The Aychar informs him that survival *is* completion: he has been re-oriented and re-employed, his seniority reset to zero. Rafe attempts to leverage the reset to cancel his accumulated company debts, but the Aychar reveals that debts carry over in full and have increased due to the ergonomic chair he destroyed in the Chamber of Avarice. A collection notice and intercom announcement confirm that Mr. Sinocarre of the January Financials has booked an immediate appointment. Rafe ignores the escalating threat, asks for his new desk assignment, pockets the re-orientation packet alongside his still-unexamined motivation poster, and walks down an unfamiliar gallery. The picaresque cycle continues.

## Ending Text

...He collected the re-orientation packet from the desk, tucked it into his messenger bag beside the rolled motivation poster he had forgotten was there, and walked out through the only door. The corridor beyond was fluorescent-lit and featureless, branching in two directions. To the left, the way he had come. To the right, a gallery he had never seen. Rafe turned right, and kept walking.

## Questions Posed

- Will Sinocarre catch up to Rafe, and what happens when he does?
- What is the true nature of the motivation poster Rafe still carries unknowingly?
- Has Rafe's re-orientation changed anything, or will the next cycle play out identically?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**Mr. Rafe Scigley:** Tall, lean, angular-nosed. Sport coat torn at the shoulder, loafers scuffed white. Bonsai root hook at his belt. Smooth-tongued, quick to calculate, quicker to pivot. Attempts to exploit every procedural detail. Fundamentally unchanged by the ordeal; his grandiose self-assurance is unshakable.

**The Aychar:** A floating mass of swollen bureaucratic flesh. Plum-colored lips, checkbox eyes that blink by darkening and lightening. Warm, procedural voice delivering systematic entrapment with paternal kindness. Processes rather than interviews. Immovable, patient, already finished before the conversation begins.

**Mr. Purslane Sinocarre (indirect):** Felt through a damp collection notice and a cheerful intercom announcement. Liquidator of the January Financials. Closing in on Rafe's growing debt. Never seen, always approaching.

## Reflection

**What worked:** The Vance-style verbal fencing between Rafe and the Aychar. The ironic reversal landing cleanly. Rafe's lateral calculation gambit and its immediate deflation.

**What was cut in review:** Placeholder cost figure replaced with specific absurd number. Em-dash corrected.

**What's missing:** Physical re-blocking is minimal; appropriate for a denouement but could be richer.
