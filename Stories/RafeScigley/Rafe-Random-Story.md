# The "Xedilian" Story

## Scenes

### Scene 1

While passing through a Cubicle Garden in the department of Gender-Appropriate Commination, Teams 7 through 8G, Rafe attempts to steal a Forever World from one of the employees (he has no 'capacity' to build his own); by the time he gets access to the employee's unlocked terminal, the session has logged out due to inactivity; instead, ray sees an advertisement for new hires in the department; he comes up with a scheme to apply and fail the interview; when he accidentally speaks the word of power in his poster, it destroys his plan without his knowing; altering the geometry of the building so that he wanders directly into the orientation ceremony by HR. Before leaving the cubicle, Rafe pockets the employee's badge - a laminated ID card left on the desk. This ID becomes important in the Chamber of Conversion.

### Scene 2

Rafe poses as a candidate, thinking to simply fail the interview and be removed from the premises; he spends a night sleeping in a dense, humid supply-forest, getting directions from a local employee; in the morning he finds his way to what he thinks is the specific hiring department; instead of an interview, he is employed on the spot by *The Aychar*, and sent to **The Onboarding Annex**; he attempts violence to escape, but is beaten and dragged to the waiting chamber; use this scene to introduce/describe the other three applicants who were lured by **the Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise** (applicants who will be killed or 'employed' during the onboarding); also introduce **Facilities Coordinator**, who guides them from room to room

### Scene 3

**Chamber of Conversion.** The printer is already running when they enter; a "Quarterly Headcount Justification" listing one more employee than the current roster. Its interface prompts for a "New Hire ID" or "Returning Employee ID" scan. Rafe scans the badge he stole in Scene 1, choosing "Returning Employee." The printer accepts it and begins generating documents under the stolen identity: badges, org charts, benefits forms. Paper piles from an unlimited supply. The Coordinator watches, taking notes. Rafe convinces a naive junior candidate that the printed W-4 constitutes a binding offer. She signs everything; the Coordinator stamps her "PROCESSED." Now Rafe needs to move before the Coordinator cross-references his falsified badge. One candidate distracts the Coordinator with a loud dental-coverage dispute; Rafe jams the printer with a roll of toilet paper. The coordinator herds the survivors out.

### Scene 4

**Chamber of Avarice:** Rafe is tempted by the view of the highway in the chamber; he recognizes a specific lane pattern or exit sign from his own past commute; the door is locked - the caretaker suggests that the first employee to figure out a way in earns the office (and is this forced into the company), suggesting the keycard is somewhere in the crowded anteroom; Rafe plans an elaborate escape through the window, he begins dismantling an ergonomic char to repurpose as a battering ram - methodical and industrious - meanwhile the Lorre and Astor candidates search for the card; Rafe and the Lorre character distract and hinder each other; at the last moment, before Rafe can employ his plan, The Astor candidate, while the other two are scheming, swipes the caretaker's keycard, which works; she steps into the kill chamber (and is subsequently crushed and slain); the caretaker frames this as basically corporately-immodest

### Scene 5

**Chamber of Anathema** - dramatic climax

### Scene 6

Rafe survives all through chambers (by luck or cunning) without be employed; he thinks he has 'failed' and will be set free, but instead is 're-oriented' in the hiring division, as a result of having gone through onboarding; all his years-of-experience reset to zero for the purposes of company benefits; he attempts to use this to re-balance his company debts, but they carry over; he also finds out that his company debts have risen as a result of damage to an expensive ergonomic chair - and is informed that Mr. Sinocarre has booked an immediate appointment with him; rafe ignores this latter remark, asks where his new desk assignment is, leaves, and turns down a different hall; the story ends here

## Dungeon Description

### The Onboarding Annex (Overall)

A decommissioned wing of corridors and testing rooms originally designed to process - or eliminate - unauthorized demons who wandered into the corporate office from adjacent buildings. A corporate immune system: it lures trespassers inward, then either terminates them or breaks their will until they accept employment.

**The Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise:** A repurposed motivational display board that broadcasts a low hum of promise - specifically, a "comprehensive benefits package" - to lure wandering demons into the annex. Same class of artifact as Rafe's stolen poster.

**Abrupt Termination (Kill Path):** Murder. The annex's lethal option for trespassers.

**Onboarding (Madness Path):** Demons are exposed to binding employment agreements - contractual documents slid under doors, established work schedules, orientation slides - that legally bind them to the company. Once exposed long enough, the trespasser accepts a position, receives a badge and a desk, and simply... stays. Often worse than termination.

**The Facilities Coordinator (Caretaker):** Maintains the annex's mechanisms, resets the chambers between uses, and monitors trespassers' progress through the testing rooms. (TODO: name and characterize)

**Three Chambers:** Conversion, Avarice, and Anathema. Each offers the Facilities Coordinator a choice between the Termination path and the Onboarding path.

### Chamber 1: Conversion (The Printer)

A standard office printer sits in the middle of the room, humming. **Termination path:** the printer begins firing reams of paper when turned on; it overheats and detonates. **Onboarding path:** it starts printing the trespassers' names on employee badges, org charts, performance reviews, and weekly onboarding schedules - documents that already reference them as staff. They weren't hired, but the paperwork says otherwise, and the paperwork is never wrong.

### Chamber 2: Avarice (The Corner Office)

A glass-walled corner office, visible but locked, furnished with a mahogany desk and a real window overlooking a highway - the Daily Commute itself. **Termination path:** the glass walls slam inward, crushing. **Onboarding path:** a cascade of keycards rains from the ceiling; the trespassers swipe frantically at the door, but none have the clearance. Each failed swipe adds a charge to their employee account - a growing negative balance they'll need to pay off before they can ever put in their two-week notice. They can see the highway the entire time.

### Chamber 3: Anathema (The Sub-Basement Conference Room)

A sub-basement conference room filled with Skeleton Crew - bones of a dissolved department that never received the proper signatures, still contractually obligated, still seated around a long table with a presentation frozen on the projector. **Termination path:** the Skeleton Crew rise from their chairs and attack, defending the room with the ferocity of beings whose existence depends on a bureaucratic technicality. **Onboarding path:** the projector flickers on and begins playing a mandatory non-discrimination compliance slideshow - the kind HR makes every new hire sit through. It goes on forever. Slide after slide after slide. The trespassers cannot leave until the presentation ends, and it never ends. Eventually, broken, they sign the hiring paperwork just to make it stop.
