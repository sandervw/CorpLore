# The "Xedilian" Story

*A 12,000-15,000 word story.*

Priorities:
- Movement
- Bloodshed
- Description
- Character
- Subtext
- Humor
- Lore
- Exposition

## Scene 1 - "The Cubicle Garden"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Character, Humor, Description

While passing through a Cubicle Garden in the 'Department of Gender-Appropriate Commination - Teams 7.A through 8.G', Rafe attempts to steal a Forever World from one of the employees (he has no 'capacity' to build his own). He poses as a Compliance Liaison conducting ergonomic spot-checks, and directs the employee to report to a nonexistent Calibration Office on the fourth mezzanine for "corrective alignment." By the time the employee departs and Rafe gets access to his unlocked terminal, the Ward of User Authentication has faded due to inactivity. Instead, Rafe sees an advertisement for new hires in this department; he comes up with a scheme to apply and fail the interview. Before leaving the alcove, Rafe pockets the employee's badge - a laminated ID card left on the desk. This ID becomes important in the Chamber of Conversion.

## Scene 2 - "The Word Misapplied"
- **Wordcount:** ~500
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Exposition, Humor

Rafe accidentally and unknowingly speaks the word of power in his poster just as the presceding scene is ending. It destroys his plan without his knowing, altering the geometry of the building so that he will wander directly into the orientation ceremony by HR (skipping the interview).

## Scene 3 - "The Supply-Forest"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,500
- **Style lean:** Smith
- **Scene Priorities:** Description, Lore, Movement

Rafe spends a night sleeping in a dense, humid supply-forest, getting directions from a local employee. In the morning he finds the entrance to what he thinks is the specific hiring department.

---

## Scene 4 - "The Aychar's Office"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,500
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Character, Humor, Subtext

We open with Rafe already posing as an applicant, for a job in the 'Department of Gender-Appropriate Commination - Teams 7.A through 8.G'. This is the enactment of his plan to fail the interview and be removed from the premises. But, instead of an interview, he is employed on the spot by *The Aychar*. It explains to him that he has already passed the interview stage; he's late for his Rites of Integration. Rafe is sent to *The Onboarding Annex*. He attempts to fight free, but is beaten up by hiring demons, and dragged to the onboarding annex.

*During the course of this scene, introduce/describe the other three applicants who were lured by the Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise (applicants who will be killed or 'employed' during the onboarding); also introduce Facilities Coordinator, who guides them from room to room.*
1. Applicant 1 - base on Peter Lorre / Joel Cairo (Maltese Falcon)
2. Applicant 2 - base on Sidney Greenstreet / Kasper Gutman (Maltese Falcon)
3. Applicant 3 - base on Mary Astor / Brigid O'Shaughnessy (Maltese Falcon)
4. Facilities Coordinator - basically a necromancer/sorcerer; base on Barquentine (Titus Groan / Gormenghast)

**Note - The Aychar:** Most feared high-corruption demon. A floating mass of swollen bureaucratic flesh: bulging lips, distended throat, eyes like filled-in checkboxes. Drifts through HR corridors, doors opening before it arrives. Pure evil wearing the mask of procedure.

## Scene 5 - "The Onboarding Annex"
- **Wordcount:** ~600
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Exposition, Lore, Description

The facilities coordinator leads them to **The Onboarding Annex.** Rafe recognizes this (do some exposition here). It is a decommissioned vault of corridors and testing rooms originally designed to process - or eliminate - unauthorized demons who wandered into the corporate office from adjacent buildings. A corporate immune system: it lures trespassers inward, then either terminates them or breaks their will until they accept employment. It does this via a special artifact, **The Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise:** A repurposed motivational display board that broadcasts a low, promising hum touting a "comprehensive benefits package" - to lure wandering demons into the annex. Same class of artifact as Rafe's stolen poster. **The Facilities Coordinator (Caretaker)** maintains the annex's mechanisms, resets the chambers between uses, and monitors trespassers' progress through the testing rooms. There are three chambers: Conversion, Avarice, and Anathema. Each offers the Facilities Coordinator a choice between *the Termination path* (death) and *the Onboarding path* (ritually-binding employment).

---

## Scene 6 - "Chamber of Conversion"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,500
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Movement, Character, Subtext

The coordinator leads them into **Chamber of Conversion.** The printer is already running when they enter; a "Quarterly Headcount Justification" listing one more employee than the current roster. Its interface prompts for a "New Hire ID" or "Returning Employee ID" scan. Rafe scans the badge he stole in Scene 1, choosing "Returning Employee." The printer accepts it and begins generating documents under the stolen identity: badges, org charts, benefits forms. Paper piles from an unlimited supply. The Coordinator watches, taking notes. Rafe tricks the Greenstreet Candidate into placing a signature on a printed W-4. The Coordinator stamps the Greenstreet character's document: "PROCESSED." Now Rafe needs to move before the Coordinator cross-references his falsified badge. Another candidate (wishing to avoid employment too) distracts the Coordinator; meanwhile Rafe jams the printer with a roll of toilet paper. The coordinator herds the survivors out.

---

## Scene 7 - "Chamber of Avarice"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,800
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Movement, Bloodshed, Character

They enter the Chamber of Avarice; a corner-office encased entirely in stained glass, with windows looking outside (to the highway, and other offices). Rafe is tempted by the view of The Daily Commute in the chamber; he recognizes a specific exit sign from his own past commute. The stained glass door (of the stained glass office) is locked. The caretaker suggests subtly that the first employee to figure out a way in earns the office (and is thus forced into the company). He further hints that the keycard is somewhere in the crowded anteroom. Rafe establishes a new plan; get into the office and escape through the window, achieving his ultimate goal of escapign the office as a whole. He begins dismantling an ergonomic chair to repurpose as a battering ram - unusally methodical and industrious. Meanwhile the Lorre and Astor candidates search for the card. Rafe and the Lorre character distract and hinder each other. At the last moment, before Rafe can employ his plan, the Astor candidate, while the other two are scheming, swipes the caretaker's keycard. This works to open the office door. She steps into the kill chamber (and is subsequently crushed and slain). The caretaker frames this as basically corporately-immodest. He ushers them onward.

---

## Scene 8 - "The Skeleton Crew"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,000
- **Style lean:** Smith
- *Style note: write this location like one of Ashton-Smith's Zothique stories - lean into necromantic, macabre, detailed textural description - like The Dark Eidolon, or The Carnal God*
- **Scene Priorities:** Description, Movement, Character

**Chamber of Anathema, Part I.** The Coordinator herds the survivors (Rafe + the Lorre candidate) down a long, long abyss. Temperature drops. The ever-present fluorescent tubes give way to a single overhead projector casting a phosphorescent cone onto a pull-down screen. The Skeleton Crew sit in ergonomic chairs around a long conference table, placards of gold and jet in front of them, bejeweled lanyards draped over exposed clavicles. A printed meeting agenda waits at every seat; the "Date" field lists a fiscal quarter that predates the building. The slideshow begins - mandatory non-discrimination compliance training, narrated by a funereal voice. The Lorre candidate attempts to appear suave, confident, but begins to crack. Rafe notices the Skeleton Crew respond to meeting protocol; he introduces himself as an external auditor, and motions to table his section of the agenda. The Crew, bound by parliamentary procedure, must vote. While they deliberate (bony fingers tapping the table), Rafe edges toward the exit, but finds it locked.

---

## Scene 9 - "The Word Fulfilled"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Style lean:** Vance/Smith
- **Scene Priorities:** Bloodshed, Movement

**Chamber of Anathema, Part II.** Rafe accidentally and unknowingly says the word of power on his poster again (INTEGRITY). The chamber warps - the skeletons and demons remain, but the room changes to Rafe's own orientation slideshow from years ago; the projector shows a company photo of Rafe, new hire, on a yellowed, curling screen. The skeletons shudder, stunned by the alteration in location. The Coordinator fails to notice, and begins to intone a contractually-binding ritual of engagement. The Lorre candidate, traumatized by the slideshow and the skeletons, screams. This causes the Coordinator to stutter during his invocation. The Crew, their implacable routine broken, lunge and tear the two demons limb-from-limb. Rafe, already near the door (now unlocked since the room is now in a past/future-state, where Rafe has already been employed), just escapes - and manages to recover his bonsai root hook too.

---

## Scene 10 - "Re-Orientation"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Character, Humor, Subtext

Rafe has now survived all three chambers (by luck or cunning) without being employed. He thinks he has 'failed' and will be set free, but instead is 're-oriented' by the hiring division, specifically *The Aychar* as a result of having gone through onboarding. All Rafe's years-of-experience reset to zero for the purposes of company benefits; he attempts to use this to re-balance his company debts, but they carry over. He also finds out that his company debts have risen as a result of wreckage to an expensive ergonomic chair (scene 7) - and he is informed that Mr. Sinocarre has booked an immediate appointment with him. Rafe ignores this latter remark, asks where his new desk assignment is, leaves, and turns down a different gallery. The story ends here.
