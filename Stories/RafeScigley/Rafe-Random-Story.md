## Scene 1 - "The Cubicle Garden"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Break after:** None
- **Style lean:** Vance
- **Scene Priorities:** Character, Humor, Description

While passing through a Cubicle Garden in the 'Department of Gender-Appropriate Commination - Teams 7.A through 8.G', Rafe attempts to steal a Forever World from one of the employees (he has no 'capacity' to build his own). He poses as a Compliance Liaison conducting ergonomic spot-checks, and directs the employee to report to a nonexistent Calibration Office on the fourth mezzanine for "corrective alignment." By the time the employee departs and Rafe gets access to his unlocked terminal, the Ward of User Authentication has faded due to inactivity. Instead, Rafe sees an advertisement for new hires in this department; he comes up with a scheme to apply and fail the interview. Before leaving the alcove, Rafe pockets the employee's badge - a laminated ID card left on the desk. This ID becomes important in the Chamber of Conversion.

## Scene 5 - "The Onboarding Annex"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Break after:** Hard divider
- **Style lean:** Vance

The facilities coordinator leads them to **The Onboarding Annex.** Rafe recognizes this (do some exposition here). It is a decommissioned vault of corridors and testing rooms originally designed to process - or eliminate - unauthorized demons who wandered into the corporate office from adjacent buildings. A corporate immune system: it lures trespassers inward, then either terminates them or breaks their will until they accept employment. It does this via a special artifact, **The Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise:** A repurposed motivational display board that broadcasts a low, promising hum touting a "comprehensive benefits package" - to lure wandering demons into the annex. Same class of artifact as Rafe's stolen poster. **The Facilities Coordinator (Caretaker)** maintains the annex's mechanisms, resets the chambers between uses, and monitors trespassers' progress through the testing rooms. There are three chambers: Conversion, Avarice, and Anathema. Each offers the Facilities Coordinator a choice between *the Termination path* (death) and *the Onboarding path* (ritually-binding employment).

## Scene 6 - "Chamber of Conversion"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,500
- **Break after:** Hard divider
- **Style lean:** Vance

The coordinator leads them into **Chamber of Conversion.** The printer is already running when they enter; a "Quarterly Headcount Justification" listing one more employee than the current roster. Its interface prompts for a "New Hire ID" or "Returning Employee ID" scan. Rafe scans the badge he stole in Scene 1, choosing "Returning Employee." The printer accepts it and begins generating documents under the stolen identity: badges, org charts, benefits forms. Paper piles from an unlimited supply. The Coordinator watches, taking notes. Rafe convinces a naive junior candidate that the printed W-4 constitutes a binding offer. She signs everything; the Coordinator stamps her "PROCESSED." Now Rafe needs to move before the Coordinator cross-references his falsified badge. One candidate distracts the Coordinator with a loud dental-coverage dispute; Rafe jams the printer with a roll of toilet paper. The coordinator herds the survivors out.

## Scene 7 - "Chamber of Avarice"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,800
- **Break after:** Hard divider (tonal shift)
- **Style lean:** Vance

Rafe is tempted by the view of the highway in the chamber; he recognizes a specific lane pattern or exit sign from his own past commute; the threshold is locked - the caretaker suggests that the first employee to figure out a way in earns the office (and is thus forced into the company), suggesting the keycard is somewhere in the crowded anteroom; Rafe plans an elaborate escape through the window, he begins dismantling an ergonomic chair to repurpose as a battering ram - methodical and industrious - meanwhile the Lorre and Astor candidates search for the card; Rafe and the Lorre character distract and hinder each other; at the last moment, before Rafe can employ his plan, the Astor candidate, while the other two are scheming, swipes the caretaker's keycard, which works; she steps into the kill chamber (and is subsequently crushed and slain); the caretaker frames this as basically corporately-immodest.

## Scene 8 - "The Skeleton Crew"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,500
- **Break after:** None (continuous action into Scene 9)
- **Style lean:** Smith
- *Style note: write this location like one of Ashton-Smith's Zothique stories - lean into necromantic, macabre, detailed textural description - like The Dark Eidolon, or The Carnal God*

**Chamber of Anathema, Part I** - the trap. The Coordinator herds the survivors (Rafe + one or two remaining candidates) down a long, long abyss. Temperature drops. Fluorescent tubes give way to a single overhead projector casting a phosphorescent cone onto a pull-down screen. The Skeleton Crew sit in ergonomic chairs around a long conference table, placards of gold and jet in front of them, bejeweled lanyards draped over exposed clavicles. A printed meeting agenda waits at every seat; the "Date" field lists a fiscal quarter that predates the building. The slideshow begins - mandatory non-discrimination compliance training, narrated by a funereal voice. She's lost. Rafe notices the Skeleton Crew respond to meeting protocol; he introduces himself as an external auditor, and motions to table his section of the agenda. The Crew, bound by parliamentary procedure, must vote. While they deliberate (bony fingers tapping the table), he edges toward the exit, but finds it locked.

## Scene 9 - "The Word Fulfilled"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Break after:** Hard divider
- **Style lean:** Vance/Smith

**Chamber of Anathema, Part II** - the word and the escape. Rafe accidentally says the word of power again. His poster activates. The chamber warps - the skeletons and demons remain, but the room changes to Rafe's own orientation slideshow from years ago; the projector shows a company photo of Rafe, new hire, on a yellowed, curling screen. The skeletons shudder, stunned by the alteration in location; the Coordinator fails to notice, and begins to intone a contractually-binding ritual of engagement. The Lorre candidate, traumatized by the slideshow and the skeletons, screams. The Coordinator stutters during his invocation; the Crew, their implacable routine broken, lunge and tear the demons limb-from-limb. Rafe, already near the door (now unlocked since the room has changed), just escapes.

## Scene 10 - "Re-Orientation"
- **Wordcount:** ~1,200
- **Break after:** End
- **Style lean:** Vance

Rafe survives all three chambers (by luck or cunning) without being employed; he thinks he has 'failed' and will be set free, but instead is 're-oriented' in the hiring division, as a result of having gone through onboarding; all his years-of-experience reset to zero for the purposes of company benefits; he attempts to use this to re-balance his company debts, but they carry over; he also finds out that his company debts have risen as a result of wreckage to an expensive ergonomic chair - and is informed that Mr. Sinocarre has booked an immediate appointment with him; Rafe ignores this latter remark, asks where his new desk assignment is, leaves, and turns down a different galley; the story ends here.
