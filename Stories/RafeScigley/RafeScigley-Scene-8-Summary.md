# Rafe Scigley: Scene 8 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

The Coordinator leads Rafe and the Lorre candidate down a long descent into the Chamber of Anathema -- an ancient conference room far below the building's known architecture. The Skeleton Crew, Contractually Obligated undead, sit around a black-lacquered table attending an eternal meeting. A compliance training slideshow drones on. Rafe recognizes they are bound by parliamentary procedure and exploits this: he introduces himself as an external auditor and moves to table an agenda item, forcing a formal vote. While the skeletons deliberate -- tapping bony fingers for aye or nay -- Rafe slips toward the exit. The door is locked, sealed by something older than hardware. The vote concludes. The Lorre candidate sits frozen, cracking. The Coordinator announces the meeting will continue.

## Ending Text

...The Lorre candidate had not moved from his chair. The compliance slides cycled past him. His eyes were fixed on the screen, reflecting the projector's glow. He was no longer useful.

The Coordinator's pen scratched against his clipboard. The next slide advanced.

## Questions Posed

- How will Rafe escape a locked room filled with undead bound by parliamentary procedure?
- The Lorre candidate is breaking -- will his composure collapse entirely, and what consequences will that trigger?
- The Coordinator said "the meeting will continue" -- what happens when the agenda reaches its end?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**The Skeleton Crew:** Twelve to fourteen Contractually Obligated undead seated in ergonomic chairs around a conference table. Bejeweled lanyards over exposed clavicles, placards of gold and jet, yellowed phalanges bearing tarnished rings. Bound by parliamentary procedure; they vote by tapping the table in sequence.

**Rafe Scigley:** Bruised and wary from prior chambers but mentally sharp. Identifies the Crew's procedural weakness and exploits it with professional composure -- posing as an external auditor. His gambit is clever but insufficient; the door is locked.

**The Lorre Candidate:** Small, soft-faced, dark liquid eyes. His composure, maintained through earlier chambers, is visibly breaking. Trembling hands, thin wheedling voice. By scene's end he sits frozen, staring, reflecting the slideshow in his wide-open eyes.

**The Facilities Coordinator:** Gaunt, iron-keyed, clipboard in hand. Dry and procedural. Acknowledges Rafe's parliamentary motion without surprise, announces the vote requirement, and closes the scene by declaring the meeting will continue.

## Reflection

**What worked:** The descent sequence, the image-sound counterpoint (compliance narration over skeletons), and the parliamentary procedure gambit. The tapping-vote percussion is the scene's best invented detail.

**What was cut in review:** Nothing major -- the draft ran clean. Added a voice descriptor for the Lorre candidate.

**What's missing:** No traded objects. The Coordinator could use one more line of characterization.
