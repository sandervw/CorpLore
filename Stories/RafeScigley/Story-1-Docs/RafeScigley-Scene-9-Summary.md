# RafeScigley: Scene 9 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

Rafe, trapped in the Chamber of Anathema with the Lorre candidate and the Skeleton Crew, unknowingly speaks the word of power -- INTEGRITY -- as a sardonic aside. The chamber warps: the compliance slideshow is replaced by Rafe's own orientation photo from years ago, and the walls resolve into a past version of the room. The Skeleton Crew, their eternal routine shattered by the spatial disruption, shudder in their chairs. The Coordinator attempts the contractual binding invocation regardless, but the Lorre candidate -- paralyzed and broken -- screams, causing the Coordinator to stutter mid-formula. The Crew, their parliamentary procedure violated, rise and tear both the Coordinator and the Lorre candidate apart. Rafe recovers his bonsai root hook from the table wreckage. The exit door unlocks -- the room now exists in a temporal state where Rafe was already employed -- and he escapes into darkness.

## Ending Text

...He pulled the handle. Cold air and the faint hum of fluorescent tubes, somewhere far above.

Rafe stepped through, and dragged the door shut.

## Questions Posed

- Rafe still does not know the poster's word has power. How many more times can he speak it before the consequences become undeniable -- or catastrophic?
- He survived the Annex uncontracted, but what does the system do with a demon who passed onboarding without being employed?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**Rafe Scigley:** Tall, lean, angular-nosed, droll mouth, rumpled sport coat. Sardonic, resourceful, self-interested. Escaped the Annex by luck and timing rather than heroism. Recovered his bonsai root hook. Still unaware the word of power is his doing.

**The Lorre Candidate:** Small, soft-faced, dark liquid eyes. Broken by the ordeal -- frozen in his chair, reflecting the slideshow. His involuntary scream disrupted the Coordinator's invocation, inadvertently saving Rafe. Killed by the Skeleton Crew.

**The Facilities Coordinator:** Gaunt, towering, threadbare custodial robe, leather clipboard. Spoke with funereal indifference. Attempted the binding ritual despite the room-warp. His stutter broke the formula. Killed by the Skeleton Crew.

**The Skeleton Crew:** Twelve-to-fourteen Contractually Obligated undead. Bejeweled lanyards, tarnished rings, placards of gold and jet. Bound by parliamentary procedure. When procedure was violated, they responded with ancient, implacable violence.

## Reflection

**What worked:** The crosscut between Rafe's escape and the violence behind him. The word of power delivery felt casual and earned. The door "forgetting" it was locked.

**What was cut in review:** Nothing cut; two lines added for bloodshed and counterpoint.

**What's missing:** Rafe's internal response to seeing his own past photo could be deeper, but restraint fits the character.
