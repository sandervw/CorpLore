# Xedilian: Scene 7 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

The Coordinator leads the three surviving applicants into the Chamber of Avarice: a stained-glass anteroom overlooking a corner office with windows onto the highway. Rafe sees The Daily Commute and resolves to break through. The Coordinator announces that the first to enter the office claims it. Rafe dismantles an ergonomic chair for a battering ram while the Lorre candidate searches for the keycard, then tries to block Rafe's path. While both are occupied, the Astor candidate steals the Coordinator's keycard, opens the door, and steps through. The office kills her instantly. Rafe and the Lorre candidate, tangled together at the closing door, are left behind. The Coordinator calls the death "corporately immodest" and ushers the two survivors onward to the final chamber.

## Ending Text

...The Coordinator retrieved his keys from the keycard reader, clipped them back to his belt, and made a final note on his clipboard. He turned toward the far door.

"This way, please. One chamber remains."

## Questions Posed

- Rafe's coat is torn; does the messenger bag (and the poster inside) survive intact into the final chamber?
- The Lorre candidate grabbed Rafe to stop him. Is his instinct now to cling to Rafe or to betray him?
- Rafe destroyed an ergonomic chair. What will this cost him?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**Rafe Scigley:** Lean, angular, rumpled sport coat now torn at one inside pocket. Unusually industrious in this scene, driven by the visible proximity of escape. His resourcefulness is real but his greed undercuts him; he builds the right tool and loses his window to someone who simply acted first.

**The Lorre Candidate (Applicant 1):** Small, soft-faced, dark liquid eyes. Increasingly erratic. Alternates between searching, scheming, and clinging to whoever seems likeliest to survive. His crumpled resume falls from his pocket unnoticed. By scene's end, he is physically entangled with Rafe, bound to him by mutual failure.

**The Astor Candidate (Applicant 3):** Composed she-demon, leather portfolio held like a buckler. Says nothing throughout. Watches, measures distances, and acts with decisive economy when the moment arrives. Her boldness and her silence are the same quality. Killed by the chamber.

**The Facilities Coordinator:** Gaunt, towering, threadbare custodial robe. Iron keys at belt, leather clipboard in hand. Delivers the chamber's rules with rehearsed indifference, takes notes during violence, and frames the Astor candidate's death as a breach of corporate modesty.

## Reflection

**What worked:** The crosscut structure between Rafe's dismantling and the Astor candidate's silent observation. The kill is offscreen but visceral through sound alone. Rafe's dialogue lands with Vancian drollness.

**What was cut in review:** Nothing cut; one traffic-hum callback added to sustain the counterpoint through the conflict.

**What's missing:** The Astor candidate's interiority is deliberately absent, but a single telling physical detail at the moment of her death (the portfolio hitting the floor, for instance) might sharpen the impact.
