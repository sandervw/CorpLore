# Rafe Scigley: Scene 6 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

The Coordinator unlocks the Chamber of Conversion and ushers the four applicants inside. A printer is already running, generating integration documents for whoever scans an ID at its terminal. Rafe scans his stolen badge as a "Returning Employee," triggering a flood of paperwork under the original owner's name. While the Lorre candidate's mounting panic draws the Coordinator's attention, Rafe convinces the Greenstreet candidate to sign a W-4 as a "witness." The Coordinator stamps the document PROCESSED, binding the Greenstreet candidate to the position. Before the Coordinator can cross-reference Rafe's falsified badge against the scan record, Rafe jams the printer with a roll of toilet paper, destroying the evidence trail. The Coordinator herds the remaining three applicants through the far door. The Greenstreet candidate remains behind, employed.

## Ending Text

...The Greenstreet candidate did not follow. He remained in the chamber, PROCESSED, his signet ring glinting under the dead fluorescents as the door closed between them.

## Questions Posed

- Has the Coordinator noted Rafe's falsified badge, or merely filed it for later reckoning?
- Will the Lorre candidate's unraveling make him an asset or a liability in the next chamber?
- What does the Astor candidate's silence cost her?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**Rafe Scigley:** Battered, disarmed, still wearing his rumpled sport coat with too many pockets. Quick-thinking, verbally smooth, willing to exploit anyone nearby. Scanned a stolen badge and talked the Greenstreet candidate into signing his own employment. Already stepping back before the ink dried.

**Greenstreet Candidate:** Corpulent, signet-ringed, fills spaces like liquid settling. Genial voice, dangerous intelligence behind small eyes. Signed the W-4 half-knowingly, smiled through the realization. His parting line suggests he will remember the trick.

**Lorre Candidate:** Small, dark-eyed, fingers perpetually folding his crumpled resume. Whispers escalated from nervous tic to audible distress. His panic inadvertently provided the distraction Rafe needed.

**Astor Candidate:** Composed, silent, leather portfolio held like armor. Positioned herself in the far corner and observed. Did nothing. Revealed nothing.

**Facilities Coordinator:** Gaunt, towering, threadbare custodial robe, iron keys at belt. Black leather clipboard. Stamps documents with mechanical indifference. Herded survivors onward after the printer jammed.

## Reflection

**What worked:** Vancian dialogue, the crosscut timing between Rafe's con and Lorre's distraction, the toilet-paper-as-weapon payoff.

**What was cut in review:** Explanatory paragraph spelling out why Rafe needed the signature. Subtext is stronger without it.

**What's missing:** The Astor candidate is nearly invisible. Deliberate, but could be a missed opportunity.
