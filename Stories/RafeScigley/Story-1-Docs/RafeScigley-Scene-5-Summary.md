# RafeScigley: Scene 5 - Post-Scene Summary

## Scene Summary

The Coordinator leads Rafe and three applicants down a narrowing corridor into the Onboarding Annex -- a decommissioned trap-vault designed as a corporate immune system, built to lure unauthorized demons inward and either terminate or employ them. Rafe recognizes what the annex is. At the junction, they find three numbered chamber doors and the Performance Panel of Ringing Sunrise, the lure-artifact that drew the other applicants. The Coordinator announces three chambers -- Conversion, Avarice, Anathema -- each offering onboarding or termination. The Lorre-type nervously questions the meaning of "termination." The Greenstreet-type watches with amusement. The Astor-type silently measures the distance to the door. Rafe, battered and disarmed, feels the Panel's pull despite knowing it's a trap. He walks through the first door.

## Ending Text

...He adjusted the strap of his messenger bag, and walked through.

## Questions Posed

- What awaits in the Chamber of Conversion -- and can Rafe use the stolen badge to his advantage?
- What is the Astor-candidate protecting in her portfolio?
- Will the Coordinator's indifference hold, or does he have a preference for termination?

## Characters Introduced or Referenced

**Rafe Scigley:** Battered, disarmed, calculating. Ribs aching from the hiring demons. Recognizes the annex for what it is but walks in anyway. Feels the Panel's pull despite knowing it's a lie -- his greed is a crack in his professional composure.

**Facilities Coordinator:** Gaunt, towering, ancient. Iron keys at his belt, black leather clipboard in hand. Moves with dry precision, never looks back. Announces the chambers' rules with the indifference of a demon who has done this many times.

**Lorre-type Applicant:** Small, soft-faced, liquid eyes. Folds and refolds a crumpled resume. Whispering voice. Asks the question everyone is thinking -- "termination of employment?" -- and receives no answer.

**Greenstreet-type Applicant:** Enormous, broad, well-fed. Laughs like furniture dragged across stone. Watches the proceedings with genial amusement, hands folded, settling in as if for a show.

**Astor-type Applicant:** Composed, silent she-demon. Leather portfolio held close. Cool eyes measuring the distance from clipboard to door. Says nothing. Reveals nothing.

## Reflection

**What worked:** The Vancian exposition voice; the Panel as image-sound counterpoint; the Lorre-type's naive question landing the scene's darkest beat.

**What was cut in review:** Nothing removed; additions made to address Rafe's physical state, Greenstreet's character, and Rafe's vulnerability to the Panel.

**What's missing:** The Astor-candidate's hidden agenda remains unaddressed -- by design, for later payoff.
