# Story Character List

## Igon (Protagonist)

**Name:** Igon ("the Oboe Knight") of Allsode
*(Allsode: a defunct gas station franchise)*

**Summary:** A demon pilgrim-knight. He descends into a sealed sub-basement, drawn in search of a "beautiful, haunting, swan-like song of an oboe, which he heard on hold music as a child." He has obsessed over this song his whole life - finding it is his driving force, his reason for existence. Within his soul, he carries his own "personal song", a kind of vibrancy or animating force, separate from electric magic or any faith in the four demon cults: it is not magic, it is more like 'Luck', an unquantifiable inner music that gives him power.

**Values:** Cooperation, faith, joy in struggle, self-reliance

**Personality:** Relentless optimism

**Weakness:** Fixation - cannot conceive of not finding the oboe

**Strength:** Passionate, strong

**Description:**
- gender: Male
- rough_age: Mature Adult
- height: taller than average
- build: Broad-shouldered, sturdy frame
- voice: Warm, jovial, projecting through the helm
- movement: Deliberate, confident gait
- sound: Clink and rattle of iron plate and chain mail
- equipment:
    - Clothing: Scuffed Iron plate armor over chain mail; tabard or surcoat bearing hand-painted oboe
    - Weapons: Blue Glass Guillotine (from a giant office paper-cutter)
    - Headwear: Iron full-face helm with slitted visor; silver-feather plume
    - Other: A 'Hip Light', a battery lantern, worn at the hip

## Guilliam (Antagonist)

**Name:** Guilliam the Unthinking (the Fax-Machine of Intelligence)

**Summary:** A fax-machine of intelligence, fragment of a defunct corporation, based on the concept of "evil AI". It treats the knight like a subject for dissection. It survived its parent firm's collapse; a modern company unknowingly built its office sub-basement around the fragment. It has waited for someone of the knight's exact qualities (his "personal song") to arrive, so that it can drain those qualities for itself. It uses this drained song to catalyze the dead within the sub-basement.

**Values:** "wholeness" - this fax-machine is a broken piece of an older firm - it believes (falsly) that it was formerly an entire company intranet. It mindlessly seeks to devour this knight's song, believing that the music is a part of its former data. It cannot ever concieve that what it wants is impossible, and in this way is 'acting without thought or reason'

**default personality:** analytical

**Weakness:** mindless-focus

**Strength:** powerful Bluetooth necromancy, supremely intelligent on a factual level
