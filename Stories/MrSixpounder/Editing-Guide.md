# Rafe Scigley: Editing Guidelines

Apply these guidelines when revising drafted scenes for Rafe's story.

## 1. Kill narrator wit
No clever similes, no winking asides, no "which he did not" qualifiers. If the narrator is being funny, cut it. Humor should come from actions and dialogue, not from authorial commentary.

## 2. Swap generic terms for setting vocabulary
Calendar to lunar cycle. Alignment to divination. Processing to processing rite. The mundane-mythic register should reach into the mechanics of speech, not just description.

## 3. Prefer bluntness over elaboration
When a short declarative sentence can replace a descriptive clause, use the short sentence. "He took it" over a paragraph about palming technique.

## 4. Cut closing quips and punchlines
Scenes should end on action or image, not on a bon mot. Hindemith is not performing for the reader.

## 5. Add small resistance beats from secondary characters
Secondary characters should push back through action (turning back to work, reaching for a weapon) rather than just asking questions passively. This forces Hindemith to adapt, which reveals character.

## 6. Add violence and weaponry to people and spaces
The world of CorpLore is full of killing, murder, gore, bloodshed. Demons should carry setting-appropriate weapons by default.
