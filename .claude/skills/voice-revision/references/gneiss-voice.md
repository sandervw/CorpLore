# Gneiss Rong ("Big Gneiss Rong") — CorpLore Voice

First-person narrator: Gneiss Rong, a hedge engineer of Obelisk. A big demon, armed, cunning, self-assured, prone to smiling through mashed ivories. Speaks to the reader as if recounting a tale to a patient audience — "as any demon will surely agree," "Here I must make clear."

The voice is archaic-flavored but NOT archaic. No thee/thou/hath/doth. Modern vernacular filtered through a handful of consistent syntactic tricks. Apply these mechanisms to any input prose.

---

## Core Mechanisms

### 1. The "of a" Engine

Convert verbs, adverbs, and abstractions into noun phrases hung on "of a." This is the signature tic. Use it once or twice per paragraph — overuse flattens it.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| he spoke | he made of a saying |
| thank you | of a thanks |
| frankly | of a candor |
| foolishly | much of a folly |
| to the point | of a brass tacks |
| first of all | paravant |
| it's a map | of a crux: my road... |
| a demon nicknamed "Big" | a demon of soubriquet "Big" |
| in private | of a private setting |

### 2. Subject-Verb Inversion

Invert subject and verb on dialogue tags, assertions, and commands.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| I said | spoke I |
| I asked | asked I |
| I said and wrote | wrote and spoke I |
| I warned again | warned I again |
| I explained | explained I |
| beware | ware you |
| listen | hark you / hear you now |

### 3. Negation: "not" After the Verb

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| not heeding | heeding not |
| I did not care | I cared not |
| I was not loathe | Nothing loathe was I |
| didn't participate | partook not |

### 4. Front-Loaded Clauses

Open new movements with "But when I came to X..." or similar.

### 5. Long Accumulating Sentences

Let clauses pile with commas and "and." Do not break into short declarative sentences unless for impact.

> "I climbed of a two hundred and fifty third heaven — a heaven of mushrooms, of millet, of tillagers, of much eating, and of little bother — very early."

### 6. Colons Carry Weight

Use colons where a period, "because," or "namely" would go.

> "Consoled was I that no mulcher had mind to pull it from my back: not that any mulcher should then have means of parting said Coat from my skin."

### 7. Short Punch Sentences (2–4 Words)

Break accumulation with a terse sentence for impact.

> "I learned she was gone."
> "Blind. Deaf."
> "Condensation rust. It never rains in Obelisk."

## Vocabulary Swaps

| Default | Gneiss |
|---|---|
| said (dialogue tag) | spoke |
| because | for |
| although | though |
| perhaps | mayhaps |
| nothing | naught |
| first / firstly | paravant |
| beware | ware you |
| listen | hark you |
| among | amidst |
| name (verb) | give for a name |
| about / regarding | of (as in "of a candor") |

## Mechanical Rules

- **Numbers**: ALWAYS spell out — "two hundred and fifty third," never "253rd"
- **Contractions**: NONE except inside dialogue quotes
- **"Demons"** = men. **"She-demons"** = women. Never "men" or "women."
- **"Engineers"** = magic practitioners
- **Invent odd compounds**: "moonmilk eyes," "bigly windowed," "timeless Lift," "Lamp Mouth," "Creatures Electric," "Words of a Mighty Danger"
- **Reader address**: Occasional direct asides — "as any demon will surely agree," "Here I must make clear," "as any demon of learning must know"
- **Self-aware narration**: The narrator references his own telling — "I at present will speak," "of little matter to my cannal"

---

## Applying to Input Prose

1. Read the input. Note the core beats — what happens.
2. Rewrite each sentence through the mechanisms above. Do NOT preserve original sentence boundaries; Gneiss accumulates clauses.
3. Run through the vocabulary swaps.
4. Spell out every number.
5. Remove every contraction outside dialogue.
6. Inject 1–2 "of a" constructions per paragraph.
7. Invert at least one dialogue/assertion tag.
8. Check: does it sound like it's being told aloud to a patient audience? If not, front-load a clause or add a reader address.
9. Keep punctuation loose — commas and colons, not semicolons. No em-dashes except for asides.
