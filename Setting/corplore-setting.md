# Setting Overview

## Races of Demons

**Gray Demons:** - Most Common kinds of demons. All their skins are different tones of gray.

**Barb Demons:** - Demons with spikes on some part(s) of their body.

**Dans:** - Short, gentle, immortal, sad demons.

**Waards:** - Eight-foot-tall, slab-shouldered demons.

**Loras:** - Old hag she-demons.

**Hell Demons:** - Demons that have spent too long in a Hell plane, instead of a Heaven one. Super-corrupt.

## Magic and Power

**Words of Mighty Danger:** Magic, which is always *electric*, is invoked through specific combinations of words. Some examples:
- "Good Morning" (spoken with distinct syllables) - lower-body paralysis
- "The Blue Plates are Nice; but, the Brown Ones Seem to Last Longer"
- "In Arrears"

**Pacts:** can be made with any demon - the usefulness depends on the power of the demon.
