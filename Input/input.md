## Scene 2 - The Word Misapplied

**Issues Identified:**

1. **"it's" vs. "its" (twice):** "badge's separation from it's employee" and "through it's exposed tube" — both should be **"its"** (possessive, no apostrophe).

2. **Lowercase "rafe":** In the italicized thought — "*it is entirely serviceable. The sort of maneuver…*" — "rafe" should be capitalized: **"Rafe"**.

3. **Semicolon misuse:** "The corridor narrowed, almost imperceptibly; and overhead…" — the semicolon before "and" is awkward. A comma or em-dash would read more naturally.

4. **Repeated beat — rubbing eyes:** Rafe "rubbed his eyes" and then "rubbing his eyes again" only two sentences apart. remove second instance.

5. **"Five-o-clock":** Should be **"five-o'clock"** (missing apostrophe).

6. **"Motivational poster" clarity:** The furled poster has an "exposed tube," but we're told it's a poster, not a tube-mounted print. A small clarifying detail (e.g., "rolled around its cardboard tube") would prevent confusion with the fluorescent tubes mentioned in the same paragraph.

7. **Minor trope flag:** The "building rearranges itself" concept is well-executed here, but the self-shuffling directory sign risks evoking Harry Potter's moving staircases. The laminate detail helps ground it — lean further into that corporate-mundane texture.

## Scene 3 - The Supply-Forest

2. **Color contradiction**: The EXIT signs are described as emitting "cold, unwavering light" that stains mist "a deep viridescent" (green), yet earlier and later the ambient glow is consistently called "red"/"ruddy." **Fix**: replace the 'viridescent' description with a reddish one

3. **"January offense"**: Uunexplained jargon that breaks the otherwise legible bureaucratic diction. **Fix**: Clarify to "January-class offense"

4. **"widened one more"**: Typo for "once more." **Fix**: Correct.

5. **"gave onto to"**: Double word. **Fix**: "gave onto."

6. **Repeated "HVAC breathing" beat**: The vent "exhaling/drawing breath" motif appears early, then is echoed at the end ("Not the HVAC. Not the building's respiration."). Eexplicit callback is over-signaled. **Fix**: Trim the final reference to just one phrase.

7. **"encased sausages"** simile feels tonally jarring against the otherwise elevated prose. **Fix**: Replace with something drier — consistent with Rafe's fastidious voice.
